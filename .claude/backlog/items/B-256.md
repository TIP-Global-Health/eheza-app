### B-256 — Well-child registration: a birth date exactly 13 years back is offered, and then the form demands two fields it never renders — READY · CONFIRMED (anchors + boundary verified) · MED-LOW · tier 4 · R25 unit 5
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Person/View.elm:681-688` pins `expectedAge = ExpectChild` for `IndividualEncounterOrigin WellChildEncounter` and sets `birthDateSelectorFrom = Date.add Years -13 today`. `Date.isBetween` in the picker is inclusive (`DateSelector/Selector.elm:357`), so `today − 13y` is selectable. Validation does **not** use the view's `expectedAge`: with `CreatePerson Nothing` and no related person, `externalExpectedAge = ExpectAdultOrChild` (`Backend/Person/Form.elm:377-382`), so the age is re-derived — `isAdult` is `diffYears > 12` (`Backend/Person/Utils.elm:36-42`), and 13 > 12 → `ExpectAdult` → `validateEducationLevel` and `validateMaritalStatus` become `RequiredField`. But the `ExpectChild` field set (`View.elm:1073-1078`) renders only `hmis / gender / hiv / modeOfDelivery`, so neither input exists. Save then reports two errors naming raw field paths (`translateFormField` falls through to the field name, `Translate.elm:30856-30879`) and never posts.
- **Two of the four child lower bounds carry the guard and two do not.** Guarded: `:667` (nutrition) and `:771` (family encounter), both `Date.add Years -13 today |> Date.add Days 1`. Unguarded: `:685` (well-child) and, more mildly, `:588` and `:710` — those two sit under `externalExpectedAge = ExpectChild`, so they fail as `InvalidBirthDateForChild` on the birth-date field itself, which at least names the right field.
- **Reachability.** Pediatric care menu → `IndividualEncounterParticipants/View.elm:195` → `CreatePersonPage Nothing (IndividualEncounterOrigin WellChildEncounter)` → pick the earliest offered date. One specific date per day is a dead end, with an error message pointing at fields the nurse cannot see. All sites.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Give the three unguarded ranges the same `|> Date.add Days 1`; better still, derive the picker bound from the same predicate the validator uses, so the two cannot drift again.
