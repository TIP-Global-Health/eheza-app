### B-616 — NCD HIV test: a saved test whose follow-up signs are pending or absent reopens with all four questions answered No — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading · side-finding of B-595 (its review round 2)
- **Mechanism.** `hivTestFormWithDefault` (`client/src/elm/Measurement/Utils.elm`, used by `Pages/NCD/Activity`) reads saved signs with `Maybe.map (EverySet.member …) value.hivSigns |> Maybe.withDefault False`, so a missing or pending sign set fills HIV programme, partner positive, ARVs and viral load with No; re-saving stores `{NoPrenatalHIVSign}`. - **Impact.** Low: NCD has no lab-technician step, so the pending marker never arises there; only a record with no signs (legacy, field absent) is affected.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Read the four answers with `hivSignAnswer` and `maybeValueConsideringIsDirtyField`, as `hivTestUniversalFormWithDefault` does.
