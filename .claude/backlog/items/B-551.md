### B-551 — CHW location: the health centre is derived from the chosen village once, at selection, and cached; a village moved to another health centre leaves its CHWs registering persons under the old centre until they sign out — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED (code; latent: one village ever changed centre on ihangane, for 18 minutes in 2022) · DEPLOYED · B-442's trigger on the device side · R50 unit 5 (blind control #4)
- **Mechanism.**
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/App/Update.elm:1281-1293
          SetVillage maybeVillageId ->
              let
                  maybeHealthCenterId =
                      maybeVillageId
                          |> Maybe.andThen
                              (\villageId ->
                                  RemoteData.toMaybe model.indexedDb.villages
                                      |> Maybe.andThen (Dict.get villageId)
                                      |> Maybe.map .healthCenterId
                              )
                  extraMsgs =
                      [ SetHealthCenter maybeHealthCenterId ]
  ```
  Both ids are cached (`cacheHealthCenter`, `cacheVillage`) and restored verbatim at boot (`App/Model.elm:185-197, 227`). A CHW is authorised by village only (`Backend/Nurse/Utils.elm` `nurseAuthorizedForLocation`), so a stale `healthCenterId` passes every gate and feeds person creation (`App/Update.elm:335` passes `model.healthCenterId` to `Pages.Person.Update`), dashboards, stock context and the Device page's authority list. A `VillageRevision` only replaces the village in `db.villages`:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/Update.elm:8143-8150
          VillageRevision uuid data ->
              let
                  villages =
                      RemoteData.map (Dict.insert uuid data) model.villages
  ```
  nothing re-runs the derivation.
- **Reachability.** Admin edits a village's `field_health_center`; the CHW keeps working without Sign Out. Sign Out + reselect heals it.
- **Impact / size.** Persons the CHW registers get `health_center` = the old centre (wrong shard, wrong HC in reports). ihangane copy: exactly one village (nid 1639111) ever changed centre, 2022-04-18 01:14 → reverted 01:32, 4 CHWs assigned. Park candidate.
- **Already recorded, distinct.** B-442 is the server side of the same trigger (the CHW group stays on the old centre); B-370 is a nurse's own assignment not reaching the picker.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Re-derive `healthCenterId` from the village on boot and on `VillageRevision` when the user is a CHW, or stop caching the HC for CHWs and read it through the village.
