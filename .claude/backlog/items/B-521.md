### B-521 — Growth charts: the weight-for-age 0-2 chart labels its gridlines 1.40, 2.43, 3.45 … kg instead of whole kilograms — READY · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-314 (raised in both review rounds of #2448)
- **Mechanism.** `yAxisLines` (`client/src/elm/ZScore/View.elm`) builds the labelled values as `Float.Extra.range { start = minY, end = maxY, steps = round (maxY - minY) }` and keeps those on the interval. `weightForAgeConfig` runs 1.4 to 17.8 kg with interval 1, so the split step is 16.4 / 16 = 1.025 kg and every value passes the `remainderBy 1 (round y)` filter. Labels read "2.43", "3.45", "4.48" with `decimalPointsForText = 2`. Since B-314 they sit at the right height, so nothing is misread; the grid is just not on whole kilograms, and the minor lines are 0.205 kg apart.
- **Same family, cosmetic.** On weight-for-height 0-5 the 1→2 kg gap gets one minor line at 1.5 kg while every other gap has lines at steps of 2/3. Raised in B-314's reviews and declined there as outside its defect.
- **Reachability.** Every child progress report for a child under 26 months. No site split.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Build labelled values from multiples of `yAxisIntervals` between `minY` and `maxY` plus the two ends, place minor lines on multiples of the minor step, and set `decimalPointsForText = 0` for weight-for-age 0-2. Check the labels hidden by `SpaceAround`.
