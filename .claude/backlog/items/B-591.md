### B-591 — Completion report: a first nurse visit at 24 weeks or later is charged with an unfinished Next Steps medication distribution for Mebendazole the app never offers there — READY · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-579 (second blind test round, PR #2521, 2026-11-12) · not sized
- **Mechanism.** The report's Next Steps Mebendazole set (`hedley_reports_prenatal_medication_distribution_expected_per_medication_set`, `hedley_reports.module`) returns TRUE at a first nurse visit at ≥ 24 weeks when nothing earlier exists and no Treatment Review "received Mebendazole" sign is present. The app's `prescribeMebendazole` (`Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm`, `resolveRequiredMedicationsSet`) needs a Treatment Review answer, which a first visit never has, so it never offers it there.
- **Impact.** Completion report only: first visits from 24 weeks where Medication did not record Mebendazole as given (after #2521) count as missing a Next Steps distribution. Pre-existing; not changed by #2521 except that a Medication dose now clears it.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** In the PHP set, require a follow-up nurse visit (earlier nurse encounter exists), mirroring the app; add a standalone test in `run_prenatal_completion_tests.php`.
