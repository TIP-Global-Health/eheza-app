### B-154 — A child with more doses recorded than the schedule expects can never read as up to date — ✅ IMPLEMENTED 2026-08-17 (issue #2096, PR #2097, branch `b154-doses-past-the-schedule` off origin/develop @9dc7dd787) · CONFIRMED · all THREE engines in one PR
- `nextVaccinationDataForVaccine` returns `Nothing` (course complete) **only on exact equality** with `getLastDoseForVaccine`, never on "at or past it". A child holding MORE doses than the schedule expects therefore gets a next dose computed beyond the last real one: `getNextVaccineDose` hands back the following dose and the interval is added to the last date, so the due date is in the past and **the child reads off track in every month from then on, with no way to clear it**.
- Worst for zero-interval vaccines: IPV's interval is `( 0, Days )`, so the phantom dose is due the day the last one was given.
- **Live scale (ihangane.live copy, 2026-08-06) — children whose recorded dose count exceeds the schedule:** DTP **12**, PCV13 **11**, OPV **10**, IPV **3** (>2 doses), MR **2**. ~38 children today, and it only accumulates.
