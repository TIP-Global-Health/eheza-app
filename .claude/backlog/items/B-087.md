- **Cost is the real argument.** It was implemented once, in the first version of PR #2030, and was the source of nearly all of that branch's complexity — retrying only the mail step inside a queue that retries whole items needs state that survives a process death, an attempt budget separate from the queue's 1000, and care about the lease.
