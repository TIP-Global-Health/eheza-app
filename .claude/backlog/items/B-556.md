### B-556 — Prenatal weight gain (sites without Healthy Start): the expected gain is taken at the pregnancy week of the day the form is filled, not of the visit, so a visit reopened weeks later is graded against a larger target — READY · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-534 (PR #2467 review round 1), not yet verified on live data
- **Mechanism.** The non-Healthy-Start branch of the Nutrition Assessment passes today's date:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/View.elm:3252-3256
            else
                Maybe.Extra.andThen3
                    (\prePregnancyClassification baselineWeight currentWeight ->
                        resolveGWGClassification currentDate prePregnancyClassification baselineWeight currentWeight assembled
  ```
  and `resolveGWGClassification` derives the expected gain from `calculateEGAWeeks currentDate lmpDate` (`Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:1810-1830`). Every week the encounter is reopened late adds one week's `perWeek` to the target.
- **Reachability.** Same path as B-534: the nurse participant page reopens the latest nurse encounter with no day bound; on ihangane 741 of 1,079 late `vitals` saves are > 30 days after their encounter (B-534's counts; this branch runs on the non-Healthy-Start sites, not yet counted there).
- **Impact.** Leans the verdict toward inadequate on a late re-save; shown on the form and persisted as the encounter's GWG indicator (`Backend/PrenatalEncounter/Update.elm` `SetGWGIndicator`). B-552 grades diagnoses at the encounter's start date for the same reason.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Pass `assembled.encounter.startDate` to `resolveGWGClassification`; check `resolvePrePregnancyWeight currentDate` in the same view for the same question. Count late `prenatal_nutrition` re-saves on a non-Healthy-Start site first.
