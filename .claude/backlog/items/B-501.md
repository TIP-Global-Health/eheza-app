### B-501 — Statistical Queries › ANC report: a pregnancy whose EDD was exactly 30 days before the TO date still counts as active, while the ANC Contacts report counts it as completed — READY · CONFIRMED (both conditions read on develop 8fdf49ade; spec read) · LOW · tier 4 · server Elm test survey 2026-10-20 · DEPLOYED
- **Found** by the server Elm test survey (2026-10-20). Untracked before.
- **Mechanism.** The ANC report needs EDD+30 strictly before TO, under a comment that says "30 days or more":
```elm
-- server/elm/src/Pages/Reports/View.elm:1460-1470 (develop)
        ( completed, active ) =
            List.partition
                (\participantData ->
                    -- Pregnancy is considered completed if
                    -- either conclusion date was set.
                    isJust participantData.dateConcluded
                        || (-- Or it's been 30 days or more since estimated delivery date.
                            Maybe.map
                                (\eddDate ->
                                    Date.compare (Date.add Days 30 eddDate) limitDate == LT
```
  ANC Contacts (LMP = EDD − 280, so LMP+310 = EDD+30) completes it on that day:
```elm
-- server/elm/src/Pages/Reports/View.elm:2426-2434 (develop)
                        completed =
                            let
                                thirtyDaysAfterEDD =
                                    Date.add Days 310 lmpDate
                            in
                            ...
                            isJust pregnancy.dateConcluded
                                || (not <| Date.compare thirtyDaysAfterEDD limitDate == GT)
```
- **Spec.** Issue #1194 (Adam): "Pregnancy is considered active from the date it is registered in Eheza until either 30 days after the EDD OR the pregnancy was marked as concluded." The ANC report's own comment reads that as "30 days or more"; ANC Contacts (PR #1549) agrees.
- **Impact.** One day per pregnancy: a report whose TO date is exactly EDD+30 puts the pregnancy in the active columns of one report and the completed columns of the other. Display only.
