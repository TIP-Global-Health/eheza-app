
- **Spec.** Issue #1194 (Adam): "Pregnancy is considered active from the date it is registered in Eheza until either 30 days after the EDD OR the pregnancy was marked as concluded." The ANC report's own comment reads that as "30 days or more"; ANC Contacts (PR #1549) agrees.
- **Impact.** One day per pregnancy: a report whose TO date is exactly EDD+30 puts the pregnancy in the active columns of one report and the completed columns of the other. Display only.
