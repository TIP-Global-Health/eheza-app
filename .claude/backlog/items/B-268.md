### B-268 — Prenatal progress report (Healthy Start): the weight-gain chart classifies from the self-reported pre-pregnancy weight instead of the booking weight, so it can draw a different expected-gain line than the Nutrition Assessment graded against — READY · CONFIRMED (both call sites read; spec quoted) · MED · tier 3 · split out of B-235
- **Mechanism.** Issue #1604 Step 1 fixes the expected daily gain from the woman's status **at booking** — her first ANC visit, where weight, height and MUAC are measured — and that status then selects 73 g/day (severely undernourished) or 60 g/day. The two surfaces feed `resolvePrePregnancyClassification` from different weights:
  - **Nutrition Assessment** (`Pages/Prenatal/Activity/View.elm:3195-3203`) uses `resolveBookingWeight` (`Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:1576-1582`), which reads `measurements.nutrition |> .weight` — the weight actually measured at the first visit. This matches the spec.
  - **Progress report** (`Pages/Prenatal/ProgressReport/View.elm:1797-1808`) uses `resolvePrePregnancyWeight` (`Utils.elm:1509-1515`), which reads `measurements.lastMenstrualPeriod |> Maybe.andThen .prePregnancyWeight` — the woman's **self-reported weight before the pregnancy**, entered on the LMP form. A different quantity, recalled rather than measured, and typically lower.
- **Impact.** The same woman can be classified `PrePregnancyUnderWeight` on one surface and `PrePregnancyNormal` on the other. When she is, the chart's expected-gain line is drawn at 60 g/day while the Nutrition Assessment graded her at 73 g/day (or the reverse), so a weighing plots in the chart's green zone while the form calls it Inadequate. The chart also plots cumulative gain from `resolvePrePregnancyWeight` as its baseline, which shifts every point on it by the gap between the two weights.
- **Reachability.** Nurse → Prenatal encounter → Progress report, when `healthyStartEnabled features`. Live: `hedley_admin_feature_healthy_start_enabled: 1` on `ihangane` (absent on the other three sites). The chart carries an `illustrativePurposes` label, which lowers the stakes but does not make a contradicting chart harmless — the same nurse reads both.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Point the progress report's Healthy Start branch at `resolveBookingWeight`, matching the activity and the spec. ⚠ Check the non-Healthy-Start branch separately before touching it: `resolveGWGClassification` grades cumulative gain from the pre-pregnancy weight by design, so `resolvePrePregnancyWeight` is right there and the two branches must keep their own baselines.
