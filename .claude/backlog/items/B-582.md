- **Mechanism.** Both phases share one `sendToHC` measurement and the recurrent arm tests the facility only:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/RecurrentActivity/Utils.elm:613-615, 909-915
        NextStepsSendToHC ->
            resolveRequiredReferralFacilities assembled
                |> List.all (referralToFacilityCompleted assembled)
        FacilityHospital ->
            diagnosesCausingHospitalReferralByPhase PrenatalEncounterPhaseRecurrent assembled
                |> EverySet.isEmpty |> not
  ```
  A hospital "Yes", or a "No" with a reason, saved in the initial phase satisfies the recurrent task raised by a lab-found diagnosis (hepatitis B, severe anemia, diabetes, neurosyphilis, Rh-negative, malaria with severe anemia).
