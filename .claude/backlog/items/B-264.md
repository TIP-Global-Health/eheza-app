### B-264 — Dashboard monthly charts divide a case-management numerator by a beneficiaries denominator from a different population — READY · CONFIRMED (both populations traced to their server queries) · MED-LOW · tier 4 · R25 unit 4
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Dashboard/Utils.elm:1439-1443` `applyTotalBeneficiariesDenomination` does `round (100 * toFloat number / toFloat denominator)` with **no zero guard** — contrast `percentageOfTotal` (`Pages/Utils.elm:247-253`), which B-009 added and which the two `View.elm` sites now use. The numerator comes from `stats.caseManagement.thisYear` (children with a nutrition **measurement** that month, from `hedley_stats_get_case_management_for_period()`); the denominator from `generateTotalBeneficiariesMonthlyDuringPastYear` (:1256-1305), which counts `stats.childrenBeneficiaries` with `memberSince < lastDayOfMonth && graduationDate > firstDayOfMonth` — enrolment records whose query keeps only `field_expected_value2 > period start`. A child measured in month M whose graduation date has passed is in the numerator and out of the denominator.
- **Impact.** Both charts are labelled "(%)" (`View.elm:2051-2064`), so percentages above 100 are possible for a month where measured children have passed their expected graduation. ⚠ **Split confidence, deliberately:** the >100% face is confirmed by construction; the 0-denominator hazard — `round (n/0)` is `Infinity`, which then becomes `yScaleMax` (`View.elm:2100-2135`) and renders the whole axis and every bar as NaN — was **not** measured and may never occur. The all-zero `0/0` case yields NaN, which the browser treats as an invalid SVG attribute, i.e. an empty chart: harmless.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Route through the zero-guarded `percentageOfTotal`, and separately decide whether the denominator should be "children with a measurement that month" rather than "enrolled that month" — that is a product question, not a code one.
