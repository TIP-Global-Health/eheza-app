### B-552 — Prenatal diagnoses: a lab result entered on a LATER day rebuilds the encounter's whole diagnosis set at the entry day's gestational age, so an encounter-day finding is re-graded across the week-20 / 22 / 37 lines (chronic → gestational hypertension, diabetes → gestational diabetes, moderate preeclampsia disappearing at 37) and the stored set is replaced — READY · MED · tier 1 (how a diagnosis is determined; ⚠ B-364 carried this as a "clinical question, not an item" with no reason — user to confirm the tier) · CONFIRMED (code; sized) · DEPLOYED · all sites · R50 unit 3

  The EGA-gated rules re-evaluated with encounter-day inputs (BP, urine protein, glucose, symptoms) but the entry-day week:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:3301-3318
  ```

  plus Diabetes (≤ 20) vs GestationalDiabetes (> 20) (`:2947-2975`), ModeratePreeclampsia 20 ≤ ega < 37 (`:2786-2806`), SeverePreeclampsia < 37 vs the 37+ emergency variant (`:2808-2822`), the emergency set at 20 / 22 / 37 (`:2070-2160`), Hyperemesis < 20 vs SevereVomiting ≥ 20 (`:3115-3127`). Example: BP high at 19 w 5 d → `ChronicHypertensionImmediate` on the visit day; a haemoglobin result entered three days later at 20 w 1 d rebuilds the set as `GestationalHypertensionImmediate` (`diagnosedHypertensionPrevoiusly` reads earlier encounters only) and `SetPrenatalDiagnoses` overwrites. Every other age/EGA gate in the app is safe only because an encounter can be worked on its start day alone (`Backend/IndividualEncounterParticipant/Utils.elm:76-78` `isDailyEncounterActive`); the recurrent lab phase is the designed exception and was not given the encounter's date.
- **Reachability.** Nurse or lab technician → Prenatal participant → Lab results (recurrent encounter page) of an encounter from an earlier day → save any lab result. `isLabTech` only suppresses the popup (`Backend/Update.elm:8978`); the rewrite happens on the lab tech's device too. Synced-in revisions do not take this path (`:2543`), so the entering device's date decides. Recurrent Next Steps read the rewritten `encounter.diagnoses`; Statistical Queries count the stored set.
- **Size (ihangane 2026-11-02 copy; orchestrator re-ran the first two).** Prenatal encounters with at least one live lab result saved on a later day than the encounter: 3,633 (1,942 of them ≥ 7 days later). Scout: 2,502 of those cross at least one EGA week between the encounter day and the last lab-save day; crossing week 20 (hypertension class) 434; the diabetes line 421; week 22 419; week 37 204. Stored diagnoses on the crossing encounters are the post-rewrite state (`gestational-diabetes` 6, `gestational-hypertension-recheck` 5, `gestational-hypertension-immediate` 2, `moderate-risk-of-preeclampsia` 3, `chronic-hypertension-recheck` 2, `severe-vomiting-by-symptoms` 2), so which began as chronic / diabetes cannot be proved from the copy.
