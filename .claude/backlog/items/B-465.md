### B-465 — CHW Dashboard: the "Home Visit" case-management figure for a PAST month counts nutrition follow-ups created in later months — ✅ IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-13 (issue #2340, PR #2341, branch `B-465-dashboard-home-visit-past-month`, base `ddd8f5df6`, commit `b7a3a6018`) · was READY · CONFIRMED (scout scratch elm-test; orchestrator read the totals generator, the item generator and the entry filter) · MED-LOW · tier 3 · R40 unit 5 · DEPLOYED
- **Found R40 unit 5.** The AI and prenatal siblings are protected only incidentally; nutrition has no date bound at all.
- **Mechanism.** The month card passes `limitDate` = first day after the selected month, and nothing between it and the count filters on the follow-up's own date:
```elm
-- client/src/elm/Pages/Dashboard/Utils.elm:1158-1163 (getFollowUpsTotals)
        nutritionFollowUps =
            generateNutritionFollowUps limitDate followUps
                |> fillPersonName identity db
        nutritionEntries =
            generateNutritionFollowUpEntries limitDate nutritionFollowUps db
-- client/src/elm/Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Utils.elm:61-64 (generateNutritionFollowUps — resolution date only)
            Dict.values followUps.nutritionIndividual
                |> List.filter (.value >> .resolutionDate >> filterResolvedFollowUps limitDate)
                |> filterListFollowUpsSetToNotNeeded
```
  `generateFollowUpItems` (`:76-104`) keeps the earliest due date per child; the entry generator (`View.elm:480-510`) only drops a child whose last home visit before `limitDate` is on/after the follow-up. A follow-up dated after `limitDate` passes every step. The AI and prenatal entries require the follow-up's encounter to be the illness's/pregnancy's last encounter before `limitDate` (`View.elm:629-641`, `:765-777`), which a later-month follow-up cannot satisfy.
- **Reachability.** CHW Dashboard → month selector (`maxMonthGap = 5`, `Pages/Dashboard/Model.elm`) → any past month → `chwCard … Translate.HomeVisit (String.fromInt totalNutritionFollowUps)` (`Pages/Dashboard/View.elm:275-283`).
- **Impact.** Both sites; a CHW's past-month home-visit workload is overstated by every follow-up written since (which is how most are: the pane shows only the earliest open one per child). Current month unaffected (limit = tomorrow).
- **Evidence.** Scout scratch elm-test: limit Sep 1 (August), follow-up dated Sep 10, no home visits → counted (1).
