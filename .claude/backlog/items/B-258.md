### B-258 — Person edit page never fetches relationships, so after a page reload an address change silently skips the children — READY · CONFIRMED (fetch path verified) · LOW · tier 4 · R25 unit 5
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Person/Fetch.elm:45-55` `fetchForCreateOrEdit` requests health centres, villages, clinics, `FetchPerson related` and `fetchFamilyMembers` — but `fetchFamilyMembers` (:72-77) *reads* `db.relationshipsByPerson` rather than requesting it. `FetchRelationshipsForPerson` is issued only by `Pages.Person.Fetch.fetch`, the **view** page (:38-42). Meanwhile `generateMsgsForPersonEdit` (`Pages/Person/Update.elm:277-320`) derives `childrenIds` from `db.relationshipsByPerson` and each child from `db.people`; on a cold start both are empty.
- **Reachability.** `/person/<uuid>/edit` is a routable URL (`Pages/Router.elm:425`). A browser reload, a version activation, or a deep link lands there without ever visiting the person page — so the address is written to the edited person only, the dependants are skipped, and nothing on screen says so.
- **Impact.** Household addresses drift between an adult and their children — the *opposite* failure to B-065, which is 🅿 parked over the same block. Recorded so the two are decided together rather than fixed blind.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Issue `FetchRelationshipsForPerson` from `fetchForCreateOrEdit`. ⚠ Read B-065 first — the user parked that fix, and making the cascade fire *more often* may be exactly what they did not want.
