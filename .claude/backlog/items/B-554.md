### B-554 — Well Child Next Visit: the pediatric-visit date is never re-derived when ECD or a medication answer is corrected after it was saved — only the immunisation date has a recompute path, and the form prefers the saved value — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED (code; 6–25 encounters on the copy) · DEPLOYED · R50 unit 2
- **Mechanism.** `next_visit` holds two derived dates. The immunisation one is re-derived by a special case bypassing the saved value, wired to the nine `WellChild*ImmunisationRevision` branches (`Backend/Update.elm:3412-3527`):
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/Update.elm:2466-2490
                  processWellChildImmunisationRevision encounterId after =
                      ...
                                                  [ Backend.WellChildEncounter.Model.SaveNextVisit assembledAfter.participant.person
                                                      (Just measurementId)
                                                      { value | immunisationDate = immunisationDate, asapImmunisationDate = asapImmunisationDate }
  ```
  The pediatric date's inputs are ECD (`generateNextDateForECDVisit`) and the medications (`generateNextDateForMedicationVisit`, `Pages/WellChild/Activity/Utils.elm:2016-2058`). `[ WellChildECDRevision _ data ]` (`:3399-3410`) runs only `processWellChildECDRevision` (encounter warning + popup); `WellChildVitaminARevision` / `WellChildMebendezoleRevision` / `WellChildAlbendazoleRevision` have no special case and fall to `processRevisions`. Re-saving Next Visit does not help:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/WellChild/Activity/View.elm:2090-2097
  resolveNextVisitDates currentDate site assembled form =
      ...
      ( Maybe.Extra.or form.immunisationDate nextDateForImmunisationVisit
      , Maybe.Extra.or form.pediatricVisitDate nextDateForPediatricVisit
      )
  ```
- **Reachability.** Nurse saves Next Steps › Next Visit, then reopens ECD or Medication from the encounter's Completed tab (`Pages/WellChild/Encounter/View.elm:183-208`) and changes an answer.
- **Impact / size.** Stale `pediatricVisitDate` on the Well Child progress report's Next Appointment pane (`Pages/WellChild/ProgressReport/View.elm:1540-1576`); no server consumer. ihangane copy (scout): of 45,299 live `well_child_next_visit` with a pediatric date, 6 have an ECD / medication node created after it, 25 have one changed after it (upper bound).
- **Already recorded, distinct.** B-372 (month-end clamp of the same date), B-053 (ladder rung).
- **Fix shape (first guess).** A `processWellChildPediatricVisitRevision` sibling of the immunisation one, wired to the ECD and three medication revisions, re-saving `pediatricVisitDate` from `generateNextVisitDates`.
