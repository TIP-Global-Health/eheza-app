### B-572 — HIV encounter: the Prescribed Medication form cannot be saved without at least one antiretroviral, so a positive patient not yet on treatment cannot complete Medication, never reaches Next Steps and cannot end the encounter — READY · LOW · tier 4 · ⚠ PRODUCT QUESTION (deliberate since `bad4a5657`, 2024-04-21 "Task completed logic"; the treatment-review reason "not started treatment" says the design expects such patients) · CONFIRMED by reading (orchestrator traced the gate chain on ca8da2a33) · DEPLOYED where the `hiv_management` flag is on · R53 unit 5
- **Mechanism.** The form's completion counts 1 only when a medication from the two antiretroviral groups is ticked; `NoHIVPrescribedMedications` exists as a value but is never offered:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/HIV/Activity/Utils.elm:594-609 (prescribedMedicationsInputsAndTasks)
    , ( Maybe.map
            (\medications ->
                let
                    mandatoryGroup =
                        mostCommonAntiRetroviralMedications ++ lessCommonAntiRetroviralMedications
                in
                if List.any (\medication -> List.member medication medications) mandatoryGroup then
                    1
                else
                    0
            )
            form.medications
            |> Maybe.withDefault 0
      , 1
      )
  ```
  Save is disabled while `tasksCompleted /= totalTasks` (`Pages/HIV/Activity/View.elm:495-497`). The task is always expected (`expectMedicationTask … TaskPrescribedMedication -> True`, `Utils.elm:98-101`) and completed only by a stored record (`medicationTaskCompleted`, `:108-112`); Medication is one of the three activities Next Steps waits for (`mandatoryActivitiesForNextStepsCompleted`, `:280-283`), and Next Steps carries the always-expected Health Education and Follow Up (`:161-175`) and the encounter's end.
- **Reachability.** Nurse or CHW HIV encounter (flag `hiv_management`) for a patient who is positive but has no antiretroviral to record — newly diagnosed in this encounter (`runHIVTest` positive) or known positive not started. The only ways out are to tick a medicine the patient does not take, or to leave the encounter open. On a later encounter the Treatment Review offers the reason `NotTakingTreatmentNotStarted` (`View.elm:525`), so the programme model has such patients.
- **Impact.** Workflow dead end; no wrong data unless the worker falsifies a medicine. Not sized (no live environment; HIV encounters whose `hiv_medication` holds no antiretroviral would be zero by construction — the measure is open HIV encounters with diagnostics but no medication record).
