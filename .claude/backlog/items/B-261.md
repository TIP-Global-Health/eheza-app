### B-261 — Dashboard case-management table: columns are sorted by bare month number, so they come out of order when the stats month is February or March — READY · CONFIRMED (arithmetic + both siblings verified) · LOW-MED · tier 4 · R25 unit 4
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Dashboard/View.elm:543-548` (and the same shape in the `MissedSession` branch at `:477-478`) does `List.filter (Tuple.first >> withinThreePreviousMonths currentMonth) |> List.sortBy Tuple.first |> List.reverse`. `withinThreePreviousMonths` (:2582-2601) correctly wraps across the year boundary, but sorting on the bare month number 1-12 does not undo the wrap: stats month 2 → months {11,12,1} renders **Dec, Nov, Jan** (should be Jan, Dec, Nov); stats month 3 → {12,1,2} renders **Dec, Feb, Jan** (should be Feb, Jan, Dec). Months 1 and 4-12 are correct.
- **Both siblings that face the same wrap DO re-anchor:** `generateCaseNutritionNewCases` (`Utils.elm:1344-1362`) and `viewMonthlyChart` (`View.elm:2036-2044`) sort and then `List.Extra.splitAt currentMonth` / `lastYear ++ thisYear`. These two are the copies that were not updated.
- **Reachability.** Dashboard → Nutrition → Case Management with `programTypeFilter == FilterProgramFbf` (the page early-returns otherwise, `:431`). `currentMonth` is the stats **generation** month (`:436-439`, post-B-163), so it bites when statistics were generated in February or March.
- **Impact.** Headers are derived from the same list (`viewCaseManagementTable:569-580`), so cells are not misattributed — the columns are simply in the wrong order for two months of the year. No wrong numbers.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Reuse the `splitAt currentMonth` re-anchoring the two siblings already use, or map month numbers to concrete dates before sorting.
