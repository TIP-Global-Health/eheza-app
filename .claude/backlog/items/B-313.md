### B-313 — NCDA scoreboard (server blob): nothing re-triggers a child's recalculation when the pregnancy-side inputs change — newborn link, participant EDD, prenatal encounter dates — so the ANC/Newborn rows wait for the child's next measurement — READY · CONFIRMED (trigger set + resolver read) · LOW · tier 4 · R28 unit 5
- **Mechanism.** The blob reads `field_expected_date_concluded` and `field_newborn` from the `individual_participant` and `field_scheduled_date` from each `prenatal_encounter` (`hedley_ncda.module:689-722`). Triggers are `hedley_ncda_node_insert/update` for `person`, `village` and `hedley_ncda_get_triggering_measurement_types()` (`:1258-1275`) — no participant or encounter bundle — and `hedley_ncda_trigger_recalculation_by_measurement` (`:1097-1103`) resolves the person through `field_person`, which prenatal encounters do not carry. So (a) on newborn enrolment the client uploads the person POST before the participant PATCH carrying `newborn` (`client/src/elm/Backend/Update.elm:4540-4551`); if the AQ run claims the person task in between (split batch, sync stall), the blob is computed with `pane1.row1 = []` and `edd_date = birth_date`; (b) later edits to a prenatal encounter date, a soft-delete of one, or an EDD correction never recompute.
- **Impact.** Self-heals on the child's next height/weight/MUAC/NCDA/immunisation save; until then ANC ≥4 and Iron-during-pregnancy rows are stale for that child. ihangane only. The (a) race is plausible-not-observed; (b) is traced.
- **Fix shape (guess).** In the node insert/update hooks, for `individual_participant` with `field_newborn` set and for `prenatal_encounter` (encounter → participant → `field_newborn`), enqueue the same recalculation task.
