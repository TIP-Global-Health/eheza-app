### B-194 — Prenatal: EDD-null pregnancies are ACTIVE FOREVER, and years-old open encounters get resurrected for new pregnancies — READY · CONFIRMED (anchor orchestrator-verified verbatim; counts reproduced) · tier 3 · ongoing · R21 wave B
- Anchor: `Pages/Prenatal/Participant/Utils.elm` `isPregnancyActive` — active unless (endDate AND outcome), else `session.eddDate |> Maybe.map (... < 92) |> Maybe.withDefault True` — **EDD NULL ⇒ active forever** (verified verbatim; the docstring says so). `Pages/Prenatal/Participant/View.elm` `viewActions` reuses the still-open encounter and offers NO way to start a fresh pregnancy while one is "active".
- Data (reproduced 2026-08-19): **4,603** antenatal participants with EDD NULL; **2,979 unconcluded and >1 year old** (traps). Smoking gun: 66 LMP measurements dated AFTER their encounter's scheduled date (36 by >1yr, 21 created since 2025 — ongoing). Worked sample: encounter 1170209 scheduled 2021-08-24, nurse recorded an LMP 2025-09-16 into it; participant 1170208 now shows pregnancy start 2021, EDD 2026-06-23.
- Consequence: a real new pregnancy lives on a years-old participant — progress-report timeline, ANC visit counts, `field_expected` start, every per-encounter EGA = garbage; NCDA + reports consume these.
- Fix direction: consumer tolerance — fallback expiry for EDD-null pregnancies (e.g. startDate + ~10 months) in `isPregnancyActive`, and don't resurrect open encounters whose scheduled date is far past.
