### B-618 — Completion report (NCD): a recurrent test due 6 or 12 months after one done on the 29th–31st may not be expected on the month's last day — READY · LOW · tier 4 · PLAUSIBLE, app side NOT checked (review round 2 on #2564, finding 4) · side-finding of B-584
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_reports_recurrent_test_required()` (`hedley_reports.module` ≈10758) counts months with `DateTime::diff()`, which gives 5 months for 2025-08-31 → 2026-02-28.
- **Open.** Whether the app's `diffMonths` (justinmimbs `Date.diff Months`) counts that span as 6. If it does, this is the B-584 class for HbA1c / NCD recurrent tests; if not, close it.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Compare `start_date_obj >= hedley_reports_add_interval($last_date_obj, '+N months')` (helper from #2564).
