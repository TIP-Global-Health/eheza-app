### B-263 — Dashboard's copy of the vaccination-progress builder drops DTP-standalone after a fourth dose date, so the Burundi immunization donut reads "behind" — READY · CONFIRMED (anchor + both canonical copies verified) · LOW · tier 4 · R25 unit 4
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Dashboard/Utils.elm:1808-1820`: `if EverySet.size dtpImminizationDates == 3 then` — an equality test, under the comment *"This entry shall appear after 3 doses of DTP were given."* The two canonical copies in `Measurement/Utils.elm` (`:10370-10381` well-child, `:10455-10466` child scoreboard) both use `if isJust <| Dict.get VaccineDoseThird dtpImmunisationsProgress` — "the third dose exists" — with the same comment. A fourth distinct DTP date therefore silently drops the DTP-standalone entry from the dashboard's progress dict.
- **Impact.** Burundi only — `allVaccineTypes` (`Measurement/Utils.elm:9942-9947`) includes `VaccineDTPStandalone` for Burundi and filters it out elsewhere. With the entry dropped, `generateFutureVaccinationsData` (`View.elm:2868`) sees no DTP-standalone progress and schedules dose 1, so the child reads as behind on the donut.
- ⚠ **Latent, not observed.** `getLastDoseForVaccine` caps DTP at `VaccineDoseThird`, so a fourth distinct date requires a data-entry duplicate or doses merged across two participants. Recorded because the divergence from the canonical gate is real and the merge case is exactly what B-195/B-208 produce.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Match the canonical test (third dose present, or `>= 3`), or extract the shared gate so the three copies cannot drift again.
