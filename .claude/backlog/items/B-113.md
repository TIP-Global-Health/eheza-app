The merge then sets `field_deleted` on the duplicate, and the person-delete cascade (`hedley_person_content_affected_by_deletion`, bundles = pmtct_participant / relationship / individual_participant) soft-deletes the duplicate's relationships instead.
- Loss scenario: duplicate child C2 is linked to mother M, original child C1 is not (re-registration is exactly how duplicates arise). After the merge C1 holds all the measurements, C2's link to M is deleted → the child no longer appears under the mother, and nothing re-creates the link. Symmetric when the duplicate is the ADULT: her links to her children are deleted.
- Fix: in `_consolidate_patients_execute()`, before marking the duplicate deleted, re-point the duplicate's relationships to the original (`field_person` and `field_related_to` sides), deduplicating against relationships the original already has (same pair + same relatedBy), and drop self-referential ones (original↔duplicate).
