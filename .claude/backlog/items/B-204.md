### B-204 — shared progress-report pane helpers live inside the Well Child view (✅ IMPLEMENTED, issue #2119, PR #2120)
**Mechanism.** `Pages/WellChild/ProgressReport/View.elm` (3180 lines) exposes generic helpers `viewPaneHeading` (`:1597`) and `viewPersonInfoPane` (`:648`), plus `viewNutritionSigns` (`:1486`). Seven page modules import from it; four take the two generic helpers (AcuteIllness, Tuberculosis, FamilyNutrition, PatientRecord progress reports). Neither helper is Well-Child-specific, and `viewPersonInfoPane`'s only dependency `viewPersonDetailsExtended` already lives in `Pages.Utils`. The edges are one-way by construction — Well Child can never import from a consumer without Elm rejecting the cycle.
**Not caused by R22.** Pre-existing on develop; `r22-client-shared-utils-dedup` (#2113) adds NCD as a fifth consumer following the established convention. Correctly left out of that PR — folding a five-module relocation into a dedup PR would mix scopes.
**Fix shape.** Move `viewPaneHeading` + `viewPersonInfoPane` into `Pages.Utils`, repoint the five consumers and Well Child itself, drop them from the Well Child exposing list. Pure relocation — verify by byte-`diff` of the moved bodies plus `elm make src/elm/Main.elm`; `viewNutritionSigns` may be genuinely Well-Child-shaped, check before moving it too.
