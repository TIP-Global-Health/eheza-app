### B-650 — NCD referral: changing a saved "not referred" hospital answer to Yes keeps the old reason in the record — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (review round 1 of PR #2601) · DEPLOYED · side-finding of B-614 · not sized
- **Mechanism.** In `Pages/NCD/Utils.elm` `resolveReferralInputsAndTasks`, the hospital `referralUpdateFunc` clears only `referralFormHospital`. The form's `nonReferralReasons` is then filled from the record, so the save holds `ReferToHospital` beside `NonReferralReasonHospital <reason>`.
- **Impact.** A contradictory record: referred, with a reason for not referring. Readers that look at the reason (progress report, the phase rule's answer triple) see both.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Drop the hospital reason from the form when the answer becomes Yes; check the Prenatal form for the same shape.
