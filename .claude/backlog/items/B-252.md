### B-252 — NCD progress report: the Partner HIV lab row ignores its own display flag, so every NCD report shows a permanently blank Partner HIV line — READY · CONFIRMED (anchor + both consumers verified) · LOW · tier 4 · R25 unit 9
- **Mechanism.** `LabsResultsDisplayConfig` (`Pages/Report/Model.elm:12`) carries `partnerHIV : Bool`, but the entry at `Pages/Report/View.elm:992` is the only configurable row in the `LabResultsCurrentMain` list with **no** `|> showIf displayConfig.partnerHIV` — its neighbours (`hivPCR` at :991, `syphilis` at :994) both have theirs. The field is written by both consumers and read by nobody.
- **Reachability.** `Pages/NCD/ProgressReport/View.elm:210` sets `partnerHIV = False` and `:862` sets the results list to `[]`, but the row renders anyway: `viewLabResultsEntry` short-circuits to `emptyNode` only when `isResultsReviewer && totalResults == 0`, and NCD hard-codes `isResultsReviewer = False` (:200-206). It is inside the WhatsApp-shared `ComponentNCDLabsResults` block, so exported reports carry it too. Prenatal is unaffected (`Pages/Prenatal/ProgressReport/View.elm:355` sets `True`).
- **Impact.** Every NCD encounter's lab pane shows `Partner HIV | (blank) | (blank) | Negative` — a nurse could read the blank as "not done" for a test NCD does not have at all. All sites where NCD is enabled. `f0062e800` (2023-02-06) added the config field, both assignments and the row — and omitted the `showIf`.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Append `|> showIf displayConfig.partnerHIV`, matching its siblings.
