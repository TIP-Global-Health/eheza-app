The initial phase's medication list offers PrEP only for the Initial variant (`Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:1979` `resolveDiscordantPartnershipSet DiagnosisDiscordantPartnershipInitialPhase`). An HIV-negative pregnant woman whose partner is known positive and not on ARVs gets no TDF+3TC task at that visit. If no other lab is pending, no recurrent phase opens to carry it.
