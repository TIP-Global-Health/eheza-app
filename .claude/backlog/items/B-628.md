- **Reachability.** Admin › Statistical Queries › Postnatal Care, rows 2–6, with any FROM later than the child's earliest dose visit. Only FROM = launch date with TO = today skips the filter (`Utils.elm:794-799`). Row 1 (SPV within 24 hours) is not affected. A child with no visit left in the range still has `wellChildData = Just [ [] ]`-shaped data, so it is counted as behind rather than left out.
- **Evidence.** Scout's scratch elm-test in `server/elm` (deleted afterwards): one child born 2026-01-21, birth doses at the birth visit, 6-week doses on 2026-03-04, TO 2026-03-31 — FROM 2018-01-02 gives the 7–11 weeks row `1`, FROM 2026-03-01 gives `0`. The existing `postnatalCareTest` (TH-32) calls the builder directly and never passes through the filter.
