### B-163 — Dashboard monthly buckets are anchored at stats-GENERATION time but re-anchored at VIEW time → stale cache shifts whole month columns — ✅ IMPLEMENTED 2026-08-16 (issue #2089, PR #2090, branch `b163-anchor-dashboard-months-on-generation` off origin/develop @caa405b52) · CONFIRMED, LIVE · MED · B-130-class
- PHP: case-management buckets carry only `date('n', $ts)` month numbers 1-12 (+13 pre-window), window anchored at `time()` when the cron/AQ job ran (hedley_stats.module:3232-3241, :3679-3690 — anchors self-verified). Client: maps bucket numbers to concrete months using its own `currentDate` at view time (`Pages/Dashboard/Utils.elm:1236-1247, 1313-1340`).
- Differing input: stats cached in July (bucket 8 = Aug 2025), viewed Aug 2 before recalc → client labels bucket 8 as Aug 2026; the case-management table + new-cases graph shift by up to a year for that bucket. Worst on dormant health centers — exactly the ones whose caches stay stale (recalc triggers only on new content + queue processing).
- ⛔ **THE ENTRY'S FIX SHAPE IS WRONG — do NOT anchor on `stats.timestamp`.** That field is stamped at SERVE time, not generation: `hedley_stats_pull_stats_for_health_center()` (`hedley_stats.module:834-837`) does `$now = time(); $stats['timestamp'] = date(...)` and is called from **`HedleyRestfulSync.class.php:338`**, i.e. on every sync, while the section data comes from `hedley_stats_handle_cache(CACHE_GET, ...)`. So `stats.timestamp` ≈ `currentDate` and anchoring on it would change NOTHING.
- ✅ **The real generation time EXISTS but is DISCARDED.** `HEDLEY_STATS_CACHE_SET` stores `['timestamp' => time(), 'value' => …]`, and `HEDLEY_STATS_CACHE_GET` returns **only `$cached->data['value']`** — the stored timestamp is never read by anything. Correct fixes: (a) have CACHE_GET surface the stored timestamp and ship THAT, or (b) better, emit absolute year-month keys from PHP instead of bare `date('n')` month numbers, which removes the re-anchoring entirely.
- 🆕 **SIDE-FINDING (user-facing, separate from the shift):** the dashboard's *"Last updated: … UTC"* label (`Pages/Dashboard/View.elm:212`) renders that same serve-time value, so it always reads as just-now no matter how old the numbers are. That is why nobody noticed the staleness.
- ✅ **LIVE MEASUREMENT 2026-08-16 (ihangane.live) — the unverified hop is answered, and it is the MAJORITY:** case-management caches: **12 of 22 health centers generated in 2026-07, viewed in 2026-08** ⇒ their month columns are shifted RIGHT NOW; children-beneficiaries: **6 of 11 stale the same way**. Oldest entry created 2026-07-07 (~40 days). **All 22 rows have `expire = 0` (CACHE_PERMANENT)** — these never age out on their own, they are only replaced when new content triggers a recalc, so a dormant health centre keeps a shifted dashboard indefinitely.
- 📌 Concrete distortion: buckets are month numbers 1-12, so a cache built in July holds Aug-Dec 2025 in buckets 8-12 and Jan-Jul 2026 in 1-7. Viewed in August the client splits at `currentMonth = 8` (`Pages/Dashboard/Utils.elm:1236-1247, 1313-1340`), so bucket 8 — actually **Aug 2025** — is presented as Aug 2026: a full year off for that column.
