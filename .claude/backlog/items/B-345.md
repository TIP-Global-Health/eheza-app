### B-345 — Prenatal recurrent Next Steps: health education counts as complete once ANY phase-2 education was saved, so education a LATER diagnosis requires (discordant couple: positive-HIV + family planning) is never shown and reads as "not given" — the health-education twin of B-336 — ✅ IMPLEMENTED · issue #2256, PR #2257 · CONFIRMED (completion predicate and form hydration read by the orchestrator; scout scratch elm-test: task grows from 2 to 4 answers, completion stays True) · MED · tier 1 (diagnosis rule — care pathway) · R30 unit 2 · all sites; unfired so far (the 110 lab-tech partner results on ihangane are all negative)
