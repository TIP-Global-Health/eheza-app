- **Mechanism.** Pregnancy Dating is offered only while no earlier nurse encounter exists, and the LMP lookup reads only the FIRST earlier nurse encounter:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:107-110 (expectActivity)
          NurseEncounter ->
              case activity of
                  PregnancyDating ->
                      noNurseEncounters
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Encounter/Utils.elm:262-268 (resolveGlobalLmpValue)
          getLmpValueFromList measurementsList =
              List.head measurementsList
                  |> Maybe.andThen getLmpValue
      in
      getLmpValueFromList nursePreviousMeasurements
          |> orElse (getLmpValue measurements)
          |> orElse (getLmpValueFromList chwPreviousMeasurements)
  ```
  With no LMP there is no EGA, and `resolveEGAWeeksAndThen` answers `False` for every EGA-gated matcher.
- **Reachability.** First nurse visit: Danger Signs saved before Pregnancy Dating with a sign whose diagnosis needs no EGA (convulsions → eclampsia, unconscious / extreme weakness, imminent delivery, labour). The assessment navigates to Next Steps; an emergency diagnosis opens Next Steps without the other activities (`Activity/Utils.elm:1320-1327`) and End Encounter then needs Next Steps only (`Encounter/View.elm:499-501`); since B-371, Next Visit is not expected without an LMP (`Activity/Utils.elm:572-574`). Any later nurse visit of that pregnancy has no Pregnancy Dating activity. Rescue: only a first CHW visit's LMP or a Healthy Start ultrasound.
- **Impact.** A woman referred as an emergency at her first visit who returns to the health centre: BP 170/115 at a later visit yields no hypertension or preeclampsia diagnosis at all, no diabetes diagnosis, no risk diagnosis, no EGA on the report. Small population, highest-risk women.
