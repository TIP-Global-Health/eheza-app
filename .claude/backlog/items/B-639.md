Later visit C: Laboratory → lab history for A → dipstick result with glucose +2 or more → Save. ⚠ Not traced: that lab history lists the dipstick as pending in that state, and that A passes `mandatoryActivitiesForAssessmentCompleted` (B-622's trace assumed the same).
- **Impact.** Diabetes and high risk sit on A, whose recurrent Next Steps nobody is in: no diabetes referral, no high risk, no Aspirin at C or later; a diabetic blood sugar at a later visit is not diagnosed either. Both sites. B-593 sized one lab-history diabetes report ever on ihangane, so expect near zero.
- **Is it accepted?** B-622's approved criteria say a no-origin result's own diagnosis "is still recorded on the earlier visit, as today" (examples: malaria, anemia, HIV). Those do not gate a later visit's diabetes or high-risk rule; this one does. No item names the urine route (grep `glucose`, `urine`, `diabetesByUrineGlucose`). Decide before building.
