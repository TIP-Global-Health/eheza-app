### B-296 — Group sessions: a child enrolled under TWO adults is checked in only through an arbitrary head of the enrolment list — brought by the caregiver, the child can stay invisible for the whole session — READY · CONFIRMED (head-pick, check-in gate and creation path orchestrator-verified; **604 live two-adult (child, clinic) pairs on ihangane**, 2026-09-01) · MED-LOW · tier 3 · R27 unit 4
- **Mechanism.** `getMyMother` (`Backend/Session/Utils.elm:38-47`) takes `List.head` of the child's enrolment list (`session.participants.byChildId`), and `cacheCheckedIn` (`Backend/Update.elm:10315-10335`) includes a child iff THAT one adult is checked in (`-- A child is checked in if the mother is checked in.`). A child can hold two un-deleted `pmtct_participant` rows in the same clinic under DIFFERENT adults: `PostRelationship` (`Backend/Update.elm:4243-4310`) unconditionally builds a `PostPmtctParticipant` per relationship when "add to group" is chosen (`ParentOf → MotherActivities`, `CaregiverFor → CaregiverActivities`) — its queries patch only relationship rows, never checking an existing enrolment of the child under another adult (B-199(a) guards only the same-adult duplicate). `byChildId` then holds both rows in arbitrary AssocList order; if the head is the absent mother while the caregiver is checked in, the child never enters `checkedIn.children` — activity pages, participants page and pending/completed counts all omit the child, the caregiver shows checked-in with nothing to do (`Translate.CaregiverMessage`), and the child cannot be measured that day.
- **Reachability.** Attendance → AddNewParticipant with `GroupEncounterOrigin`, or any relationship created with the group checkbox; both adults appear on the attendance list (`byMotherId` has an entry per row). Population where the pick can decide: **604 (child, clinic) pairs with >1 adult on `ihangane.live`** (vhw/uvl not queried). How often only the non-head adult attends is not measured.
- **Impact.** Group-nutrition sessions; the child's measurements for that day are silently BLOCKED, not corrupted. `viewFamilyLinks` and the attendance child listing ride the same arbitrary pick.
- **Distinctness.** Not the R26-declined `splitChild/MotherMeasurements` head-pick (measurement rows); not B-198 (lost enrolments) nor B-199 (same-adult duplicates).
- **Fix shape (guess).** Check the child in if ANY adult in `byChildId` is checked in; have `getMyMother` prefer a checked-in / `MotherActivities` participant before falling back to head.
- **Sizing (run 2026-09-01, ihangane: 604).** `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM (SELECT p.field_person_target_id child, c.field_clinic_target_id clinic, COUNT(DISTINCT a.field_adult_target_id) adults FROM node n JOIN field_data_field_person p ON p.entity_id=n.nid AND p.bundle='pmtct_participant' JOIN field_data_field_adult a ON a.entity_id=n.nid JOIN field_data_field_clinic c ON c.entity_id=n.nid LEFT JOIN field_data_field_deleted d ON d.entity_id=n.nid WHERE n.type='pmtct_participant' AND n.status=1 AND IFNULL(d.field_deleted_value,0)=0 GROUP BY child, clinic HAVING adults>1) t;`
