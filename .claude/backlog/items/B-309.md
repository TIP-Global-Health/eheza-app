### B-309 — Statistical Queries › Postnatal Care: "Up to date with immunization" rows never see doses recorded at Child Scoreboard encounters, so both-programme children read as behind — READY · CONFIRMED (emitter switch + trigger list read; Elm consumer read; live-sized) · MED-LOW · tier 3 · R28 unit 4 · ⭐ DEPLOYED
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_reports_calculate_aggregated_data_for_person` collects vaccination dates only from the `HEDLEY_ACTIVITY_WELL_CHILD_*_IMMUNISATION_CONTENT_TYPE` cases (`hedley_reports.module:843-919`, keyed by `field_well_child_encounter`), and `hedley_reports_get_triggering_measurement_types()` (`:467-500`) lists no `child_scoreboard_*_iz` bundle. - **Every sibling merges both sources:** `hedley_ncda_calculate_aggregated_data_for_person` (`hedley_ncda.module:981-1020`, CS and WC cases paired), the PHP Completion engine (`hedley_reports_well_child_load_child_scoreboard_immunisations`, `:4825/:5005`), and the client itself — `generateVaccinationProgressDictsForWellChild` (`client/src/elm/Measurement/Utils.elm:10183-10225`) merges `vaccinationProgressByChildScoreboard` into the SPV history so a nurse is not asked to re-record doses the CHW captured; those doses therefore never become `well_child_*_immunisation` nodes. (Client leg: merge traced; that the SPV save writes nothing for a CS-recorded dose is the design, not re-proven by stepping the handler.)
