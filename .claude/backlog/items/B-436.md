### B-436 — Sync download: a late second copy of a download reply puts the cycle back into a lane it has already left — READY · VERIFIED (orchestrator traced; scout ran 4 scratch elm-tests) · MED-LOW · tier 3 (un-deployed: the drop branch arrived with [[B-317]] / PR #2266)
- **Found R37 unit 1** (sync layer as merged, @e747d3eaf).
- **Mechanism.** After 30 s (`downloadRequestTimeout`) the tick sends the authority/general GET again. Those GETs have no HTTP timeout, so both copies eventually reply. The identity check (`isCurrentAuthorityRequest`: HC uuid + `lastFetchedRevisionId`) cannot tell the two copies apart when the reply is empty, because an empty reply does not move the cursor. No handler checks that `model.syncStatus` is still the lane that sent the request.
```elm
-- client/src/elm/SyncManager/Update.elm:189-194, 239-244
        BackendAuthorityFetchHandle zipper requestTime webData ->
            ...
            if SyncManager.Utils.isCurrentAuthorityRequest requested model then
            ...
            else if requestTime == model.downloadRequestTime then
                dropAndRefetch SyncDownloadAuthority
```
  - `downloadRequestTime` is written only by the authority fetch (`:180`) and the general fetch (`:746`), so it still matches during the stats lane and later.
  - When the last HC replies empty, `determineSyncStatus` rewinds the zipper and moves to stats (`SyncManager/Utils.elm:305-317`: `( SyncDownloadAuthorityDashboardStats RemoteData.NotAsked, Just (Zipper.first zipper) )`).
- **Sequences.**
  - **Several HCs:** the first copy of the last HC's empty reply is accepted and the cycle moves to stats. The second copy has `requestTime == downloadRequestTime` but the zipper's current HC no longer matches, so `dropAndRefetch` sets `SyncDownloadAuthority NotAsked`. The whole authority walk runs again and the stats request in flight is abandoned.
  - **One HC:** the late copy is accepted outright. Its save ack sets `syncStatus = SyncDownloadAuthority …` (`:324-329`), which puts stats back to `NotAsked`, and a second stats GET goes out.
  - **General lane (`:759`, `:805`, `:882-884`):** the same happens. A late general confirm arriving during the authority lane rewinds that lane.
  - **Stats handler (`:419-425`):** it checks only `(Zipper.current zipper).uuid`, not the lane. An orphaned stats reply for the first HC is applied, or triggers a drop, while the cycle is in the authority lane.
- **Contrast.** The failure-path sibling already checks the lane: `Update.elm:2406-2408` `case ( indexDbSaveResult.table, modelWithError.syncStatus ) of ( IndexDbSaveResultTableAutority, SyncDownloadAuthority _ ) -> parkIfCurrentRequest`.
- **Impact.** All sites; happens on slow links (a reply taking more than 30 s), which is the condition B-317 targets. Cursors stay correct and stats land in the right shard. The cost is churn: the authority lanes are walked again, stats GETs are repeated (live payloads average 2.9 MB, up to 9.3 MB), and one HC's stats may be skipped for that cycle. ⚠ Not traced: a late reply arriving after the next cycle's uploads have started, where `dropAndRefetch` would overwrite an upload lane and a batch could be sent again (the [[B-266]] duplicate-WhatsApp shape).
- **Not a declined class.** Nothing is poisoned or stalled; a lane's state is overwritten by a reply meant for another lane.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Gate the accept, save-ack and `dropAndRefetch` branches, and the stats handler, on `model.syncStatus` still being the lane that sent the request, as `:2406` does. Alternatively, include the request time in the identity check for empty replies.
