### B-310 — Aggregated NCDA scoreboard: the shared "children under 2" denominator uses the exact birth date while every age-gated numerator floors it to the month, so the current-month column's percentages can miss or exceed their cohort — READY · CONFIRMED (both anchors read) · LOW · tier 4 · R28 unit 4
- **Mechanism.** `server/elm/src/Pages/Scoreboard/View.elm:79-80` `childrenUnder2`: `ageInMonths = diffMonths record.birthDate targetDateForMonth`; every age-gated numerator (`:196`, `:526`, `:714`, `:822`): `diffMonths (Date.floor Month record.birthDate) targetDateForMonth`. For past-month columns the target is the last day of the month so both agree (except 29–31st birthdays in shorter months); for the current-month column (target = today) a child whose second birthday falls later this month is under 2 by the exact rule (in the denominator) but 24 by the floored rule (excluded from Immunization row 1, Targeted-Interventions rows 2–3; likewise the 6-month gate for Breastfed row 1).
- **Reachability / impact.** Aggregated NCDA scoreboard, "%" mode, current-month column only; a handful of children per column. ihangane only (`ncda` flag). Predates B-128 (`b57f62fdc`, 2024-03) and survived the B-128 and R22 PR-G fold rewrites.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Floor the birth date in `childrenUnder2` too — the panes already treat "age at the column" as a month-granularity notion.
