### B-589 — Case Management: a TB or HIV follow-up of a patient who is in no village roster is listed for nobody — READY · LOW · tier 4 · ⚠ product question · CONFIRMED by reading on PR #2515's head, not sized · side-finding of B-234 (review rounds 1 and 2, 2026-11-11)
- **Mechanism.** A community health worker's panes list residents of the selected village only (after #2515 the TB and HIV panes too). The roster is an exact match on the five address fields. A nurse's Case Management has no TB or HIV pane:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Utils.elm (nurseFilters)
  nurseFilters =
      [ FilterContactsTrace, FilterPrenatalLabs, FilterNCDLabs ]
  ```
- **Effect.** A patient from outside the catchment, or whose address is spelt differently from the village record, has their TB / HIV follow-up (including the one a positive HIV test creates) in no Case Management. Before #2515 it showed to any health worker of the health centre only when the person happened to be loaded. The other six panes have always had the same gap.
- **Question.** Who should follow up a patient in no village: a nurse pane, or a "no village" bucket? Size first: follow-ups whose person matches no village node.
- **Fix shape (guess).** None until the question is answered.
