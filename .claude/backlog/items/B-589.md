### B-589 — Case Management: a TB or HIV follow-up of a patient who is in no village roster is listed for nobody — ✅ CLOSED by design (user 2026-11-23) · LOW · tier 4 · ⚠ product question · CONFIRMED by reading on PR #2515's head, sized live 2026-11-23 (1 patient in the last 12 months) · side-finding of B-234 (review rounds 1 and 2, 2026-11-11)
- **Mechanism.** A community health worker's panes list residents of the selected village only (after #2515 the TB and HIV panes too). The roster is an exact match on the five address fields. A nurse's Case Management has no TB or HIV pane:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Utils.elm (nurseFilters)
  nurseFilters =
      [ FilterContactsTrace, FilterPrenatalLabs, FilterNCDLabs ]
  ```
- **Effect.** A patient from outside the catchment, or whose address is spelt differently from the village record, has their TB / HIV follow-up (including the one a positive HIV test creates) in no Case Management. Before #2515 it showed to any health worker of the health centre only when the person happened to be loaded. The other six panes have always had the same gap.
- **Question.** Who should follow up a patient in no village: a nurse pane, or a "no village" bucket? Size first: follow-ups whose person matches no village node.
- **Fix shape (guess).** None until the question is answered.
- **Sized live 2026-11-23** (patients, all-time / last 12 months; "no village" = address matches no published village record, five names, case ignored, as `isVillageResident`; "no CHW village" = no matching village with a CHW assigned). TB and HIV management are CHW-only encounters (`Pages/IndividualEncounterTypes/View.elm`), flags on for ihangane, vhw, uvl; off on tip-somalia. The HIV pane also lists placeholder follow-ups the service worker builds (`nodes.js`) for a positive Prenatal or NCD HIV test with no HIV follow-up.
  - ihangane: positive HIV test, no follow-up 48 / 1 — no village 18 / 1, no CHW village 30 / 1; Acute Illness TB suspect 194 / 62 — no village 28 / 0, no CHW village 32 / 0; TB follow-up 12 / 8 and HIV follow-up 1 / 0 — all in CHW villages.
  - vhw: HIV follow-up 22 / 17, TB follow-up 9 / 2, TB suspect 3 / 1 — all in CHW villages. uvl: none from any source.
  - Counts are patients who ever entered a source, not follow-ups still open; a placeholder stays open until an HIV follow-up exists.
- ✅ **CLOSED 2026-11-23 by the user (verbatim: "close it as by design").** TB and HIV management are CHW community programmes; a patient on no CHW's village roster is outside them. Live cost when closed: 1 patient in 12 months (ihangane, positive HIV test, no village). Do not re-pitch.
