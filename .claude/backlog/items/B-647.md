### B-647 — Group enrolment: near a month end, a child the group still takes gets an enrolment end date of today, so the enrolment may end the day it starts — READY · LOW · tier 4 · PLAUSIBLE (review round 2 of PR #2592; whether an end date equal to the session date drops the child is NOT checked) · DEPLOYED · side-finding of B-560
- **Mechanism.** `Backend/Update.elm` (PMTCT participant create) sets `defaultEndDate = Date.add Months graduatingAgeInMonth birthDate`; `Date.add` moves a missing day to the month's last day, while `groupTakesChild` uses `diffMonths`. Example: today 2026-02-28, birth date 2023-12-31 → 25 whole months (taken), end date clamps to 2026-02-28 = today.
- **Impact.** Up to three birth dates a month, on the last days of short months. Unknown whether the session lists a participant whose end date is the session date.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Derive the end date with the same month arithmetic as `groupTakesChild`, or check the session's participant filter first.
