### B-633 — Statistical Queries › Postnatal Care: row 1 (visit within 24 hours of birth) can count one newborn in two neighbouring ranges — READY · CONFIRMED by reading (review round 2 of PR #2573; not run) · LOW · tier 4 · DEPLOYED · all sites · not sized · side-finding of B-628
- **Mechanism.** `generatePostnatalCareReportData` (`server/elm/src/Pages/Reports/View.elm`) takes each child's first well-child visit *inside the range* and counts it when it falls on the birth day or the next day:
  ```elm
  -- server/elm/src/Pages/Reports/View.elm (row 1)
        totalEncountersWithin24HoursOfBirth =
            List.filterMap
                (\patientData ->
                    Maybe.andThen
                        (\wellChildData ->
                            List.concat wellChildData
                                |> List.sortWith (sortByDate .startDate)
                                |> List.head
  ...
                records
  ```
  `records` holds only visits between FROM and TO, so the first visit in the range is not necessarily the child's first visit.
- **Example.** Born 28 Feb, visits on 28 Feb and 1 Mar: the February report counts the 28 Feb visit; the March report sees 1 Mar as the first visit, birth + 1 day, and counts the child again.
- **Impact.** Monthly figures summed over months over-count row 1 by the newborns seen on both their birth day and the next day across a month boundary. Report only; all sites.
- **Kept out of B-628.** Its criterion 7 kept row 1 on visits inside the range, as it was.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Find the first visit from the scope records (`generatePostnatalCareReportData` already receives them), then count it only when that visit is inside the range.
