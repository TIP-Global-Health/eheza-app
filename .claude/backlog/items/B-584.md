### B-584 — Completion report: a next medication or vaccine dose that falls 6, 9 or 12 months after a dose given on the 29th–31st is dated a few days LATER than the app's, because PHP month arithmetic runs past a short month's end while the app stops on its last day — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (both sides) · side-finding of B-571 (PR #2507 review round 1, findings 2 and 3) · DEPLOYED, all sites with completion data
- **Mechanism.** The Well Child medication window and the immunisation next-dose date add months with PHP's overflowing arithmetic:
  ```php
  -- server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_reports/hedley_reports.module:6042-6044 (hedley_reports_well_child_add_medication_activities)
        // The date belongs to the encounter it came from, so add to a copy.
        $next_administration_date_obj = (clone $last_administration_date_obj)->add(new DateInterval('P6M'));
        if ($start_date_obj >= $next_administration_date_obj) {
  -- :5835-5838 (immunisation next dose; intervals from hedley_reports_get_interval_for_vaccine, e.g. '+6 months', '+12 months' at :8139, :8144)
      $interval_between_dosed = hedley_reports_get_interval_for_vaccine($immunisation_type, $site);
      $last_dose_date_obj = new DateTime($last_dose_date);
      $next_dose_date_obj = clone $last_dose_date_obj;
      $next_dose_date_obj->modify($interval_between_dosed);
  ```
  2025-08-31 + 6 months is 2026-03-03 in PHP; the app's `Date.add Months 6` gives 2026-02-28.
- **Reachability.** A dose or medication given on the 29th, 30th or 31st whose next one falls in a shorter month; a Well Child (or other immunisation) encounter in the few days between the app's date and PHP's.
- **Impact.** Completion report rows only: in those days the app offers the medication or dose and the report does not expect it, so a given one goes unlisted (harmless) and a skipped one is not charged. Not sized. Predates #2490; the age counts were fixed by B-571.
- **Already recorded, distinct.** B-571 (age in months, PR #2507), B-372 (app side). The server Elm scoreboard's schedule twin was not checked. Grepped `DateInterval('P`, `modify('+`, `months'`.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** One PHP helper that adds N months and clamps to the month's last day, used at both sites (and any other `+N months` in the report builders — grep first).
