### B-507 — Completion report: Acute Illness follow-ups ignore the nurse's first encounter — ✅ IMPLEMENTED 2026-10-25 (issue #2148, PR #2419, branch `B-507-ai-completion-nurse-split`, base `d6f214265`) · CONFIRMED (code + live data) · LOW · tier 4
- **Mechanism.** The server splits earlier encounters at the nurse encounter with `+ 1`, putting it at the end of the CHW part, and uses the part after it whenever that is not empty. The app (`splitByInitialNurseEncounter`) starts the second part at the nurse encounter and does not split when a nurse ran the first one.

  ```php
  -- server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_reports/hedley_reports.module:3848-3851 (before the fix)
      $first_initial_with_subsequents = array_slice($previous_encounters_data, 0, $initial_nurse_encounter_index + 1);
      $second_initial_with_subsequents = array_slice($previous_encounters_data, $initial_nurse_encounter_index + 1);
    }
    $initial_with_subsequents = empty($second_initial_with_subsequents) ? $first_initial_with_subsequents : $second_initial_with_subsequents;
  ```

- **Effect.** Nurse-run illness, third encounter on: the nurse's first encounter is dropped, so Malaria testing is expected after a positive test and Ongoing Treatment is not expected after a prescription. Takeover rows also differ. Report-only.
- **Live (ihangane, 2026-10-25).** 59 nurse-run illnesses with 3+ encounters (5 since 2025) of 23,636. Zero takeovers. Zero illnesses with two `nurse-encounter` rows, so "first vs last" is moot.
- **Fix.** `initial_with_subsequents` = slice from the last nurse encounter when its index > 0, else all earlier encounters.
- **Discriminated in the local app (rolled back).** Three cloned encounters, fever at the third, a positive test and prescription at the first. Nurse-run: old expects Malaria / no Ongoing Treatment, fixed the reverse. Takeover: reversed the other way. CHW-only control unchanged. Harness: scratchpad `b507-harness.php`.
