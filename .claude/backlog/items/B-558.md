### B-558 — Prenatal activity page: the rest of the pregnancy-week maths still uses the day the page is opened, so a visit reopened weeks later shows the obstetrical exam's fundal-height alert against a later week — READY · CONFIRMED (anchors read 2026-11-05) · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-556 (its review round 1, finding 1)
- **Mechanism.** After B-556 the GWG verdict uses the visit's start date, but the obstetrical exam's alerts still take the week from today:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/View.elm:3500-3505
          alerts =
              Maybe.map
                  (\lmpDate ->
                      let
                          egaInWeeks =
                              calculateEGAWeeks currentDate lmpDate |> toFloat
  ```
  and so do week-based rules in `Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm` (`calculateEGAWeeks currentDate lmpDate` at ≈165, 181, 808, 1394, 6204, 6409 — not each read for what it decides).
- **Reachability.** The B-534 / B-556 path: the nurse participant page reopens the latest unfinished nurse encounter on any later day.
- **Impact.** A week-20 fundal height reopened at week 24 shows a red alert; one visit is graded at two different weeks on two forms. Week-gated task rules may also change which tasks a reopened visit expects — unverified per site.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Read each `currentDate` EGA site for what it decides, and use `assembled.encounter.startDate` where the decision belongs to the visit. Overlaps B-552's "grade at the encounter's start date" principle.
