### B-488 — Person registration GPS: a create or edit person page reached by reload or app start never asks for a GPS reading, so that registration has no coordinates — READY · VERIFIED (orchestrator read `init` and both request sites) · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-390 (review round 1 of B-390's fix) · DEPLOYED on the three `gps_coordinates` sites
- **Found 2026-10-15** in review round 1 of B-390's fix. Predates that PR.
- **Mechanism.** `getCoordinates ()` is sent only from `UrlChanged` in `App/Update.elm`. `init` sets `activePage` from the URL and starts with `coordinates = Nothing`, but does not send the request:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/App/Update.elm (UrlChanged, with B-390's fix)
                coordinatesCmd =
                    if pageChanged && gpsCoordinatesEnabled features then
                        case activePage of
                            UserPage (CreatePersonPage _ _) ->
                                App.Ports.getCoordinates ()

                            UserPage (EditPersonPage _) ->
                                App.Ports.getCoordinates ()
                            ...
  ```
  `grep getCoordinates client/src/elm` finds no other sender.
- **Reachability.** The app reloads while the nurse is on the create person page: a self-refresh after a long sync, the "Version" activation, or a manual reload. The GPS section stays empty for that registration, and nothing retries.
- **Impact.** That registration is saved with no coordinates. Nothing wrong is stored.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Send `getCoordinates ()` from `init` when the starting page is a person page. Share the page check with `UrlChanged`.
