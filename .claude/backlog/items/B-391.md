### B-391 — Eleven views label the level-5 address "Village" regardless of site, so Burundi reads "Village" where the unit's own resolver says "Sub-Colline" — READY · CONFIRMED (orchestrator read the resolver, the translation and every call site) · LOW · tier 4 · R33 unit 4 · vhw + uvl
- **Mechanism.** The unit already provides the site-aware resolver, and the three form/report surfaces use it:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/GeoLocation/Utils.elm:3147-3152
  resolveGeoSructureLabelLevel5 : Site -> TranslationId
  resolveGeoSructureLabelLevel5 site =
      case site of

  ```
