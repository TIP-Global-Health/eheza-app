### B-292 — Immunisation (Well Child + Child Scoreboard): for a child followed by BOTH programs, the form derives the interval gate and the catch-up date range from the FIRST dose instead of the last — READY · CONFIRMED (AssocList reversal proven by scout in elm repl AND re-derived from source by orchestrator; every consumer hop re-read) · MED · tier 3 · R27 unit 2 · ⭐ DEPLOYED (`mergeVaccinationProgressDicts` + consumers identical on `origin/main`); **3,802 ihangane children** hold both participant types (live, 2026-09-01)
- **Mechanism.** The vendored AssocList APPENDS on insert (`AssocList.elm:235`, update's Nothing-branch `List.concat [ alist, [pair] ]`) but `merge` (`:312-339`) rebuilds via `List.foldr` + append-insert, which REVERSES whatever it builds. `mergeVaccinationProgressDicts` (`Measurement/Utils.elm:9960-9984`) is nested `Dict.merge` with plain `Dict.insert` steps, so for any vaccine present in BOTH inputs (`bothStep`) the per-vaccine dose dict comes out dose-DESCENDING — and both program builders emit an entry for EVERY site vaccine even with zero doses (`generateVaccinationProgressForWellChild` `:10424-10438`, CS sibling `:10441+`), so `bothStep` fires for all common vaccines whenever the child has both a WellChild and a ChildScoreboard participant (`:10188-10218`, `:10296-10322`; with only one participant the other dict is outer-empty and `leftStep` passes the inner dict through unreversed). Un-merged, `generateVaccinationProgressForVaccine` (`:1270-1288`) sorts doses ascending before `Dict.fromList`, so order-trusting reads are correct — until the merge flips them.
- **The order-trusting consumer.** `vaccinationFormDynamicContentAndTasks` (`Measurement/Utils.elm:1375-1379`): `lastDoseData = List.filter (date /= currentDate) allDosesGivenData |> List.reverse |> List.head`, where the list opens with `Dict.get vaccineType assembled.vaccinationHistory |> Dict.toList` (WellChild wrapper `Pages/WellChild/Activity/Utils.elm:1789-1792`; CS wrapper `Pages/ChildScoreboard/Activity/View.elm:558-561`). Over a descending dict that returns the LOWEST dose. Everything derived from it is then wrong: `expectedOnDate` (`:1495-1502`) — the earliest day today's dose may be offered — becomes roughly one interval after dose 1 (long past), and update-mode `dateFrom` (`:1604-1613`) opens the "administered previously" calendar from that same early date, allowing dates BEFORE the previous dose (which sort-and-zip re-pairing in `generateVaccinationProgressForVaccine` can then attach to the wrong dose). Visible corroboration: the form's history section renders doses descending ("2" above "1").
- **Worked example.** Child followed by both programs; OPV1 late on 2025-10-01, OPV2 on 2026-08-15; today 2026-08-31. Merged `toList` = `[(dose2, 08-15), (dose1, 2025-10-01)]` → `lastDoseData` = dose 1 → `expectedOnDate` = 2025-10-29 ≤ today → the form OFFERS OPV3 sixteen days after OPV2 (correct gate: 2026-09-12). On-schedule infants are masked by the birth-anchored `expectVaccineDoseForPerson` limit; catch-up children — the population this form exists for — are not.
