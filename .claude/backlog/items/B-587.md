### B-587 — Prenatal: Aspirin for a preeclampsia risk is offered at ONE visit only — declined, skipped or "not given" at that visit, it is never offered again in the pregnancy; and a moderate risk that later becomes high offers it a second time even when it was given — READY · MED · **tier 1 (standing rule: care pathway)** · ⚠ **product question — the spec trail is ambiguous, ask before building** · CONFIRMED by reading on `origin/develop` b62b080eb · DEPLOYED, all sites · side-finding of B-577 (filed on request 2026-11-11: "file items for 1512 and 1529")
- **Spec trail.** Issue #1512 (original): Aspirin as a Medication-activity subactivity that "should appear for the first time at 12 gestational weeks … and should disappear once distributed". The revision comment (adamhstewart, 2025-07-02; PR #1543) moved it to Next Steps: "It should appear if the patient has a diagnosis of moderate or severe risk of preeclampsia", from ≥ 12 weeks. Whether "disappear once distributed" survived the move is not said.
- **Code.** Aspirin is offered only through the risk diagnosis, and each risk diagnosis is made once per pregnancy:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:1879-1895 (resolveRequiredMedicationsSet — the only Aspirin path)
  ```


- **Effects.** (a) A nurse who records Aspirin "not given" (out of stock, patient declined) at the diagnosing visit is never offered it at a later visit — the diagnosis is already in the past. (b) Moderate risk at visit 1 (Aspirin given), high risk at a later visit (e.g. hypertension found): the high-risk diagnosis is new, so Aspirin is offered again although the woman is already on it.
- **Interaction.** B-579 (Medication activity offered at the first nurse visit only) is the same "once and never again" shape for supplements; B-577 (recurrent-phase misfiling) currently makes (a) happen without any decline. Present after B-577 merges.
- **Fix shape (first guess, only after the product answer).** Offer Aspirin at any visit ≥ 12 weeks while a risk diagnosis exists in the pregnancy and no Aspirin distribution has been recorded; that also covers (b). Needs a reader over previous encounters' medication distributions.
