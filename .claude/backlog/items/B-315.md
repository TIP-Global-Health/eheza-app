### B-315 — Growth charts: a measurement outside the chart's x-range is pinned to the frame edge instead of dropped, so a 24–26-month-old's latest points are drawn against the 24-month reference and read ~0.25 z healthier — ✅ IMPLEMENTED 2026-11-01, issue #2451, PR #2452, branch `B-315-growth-chart-edge-points` · CONFIRMED (clamp, no child-point range filter, 26-month selection rule all read; live-sized) · MED-LOW · tier 3 · R28 unit 6 · ⭐ DEPLOYED (all sites)
- **Mechanism.** `plotPoints` clamps both axes to the output box (`client/src/elm/ZScore/View.elm:590-599`: `plotX x = … |> clamp config.output.minX config.output.maxX`, same for `plotY`). Reference curves are filtered to the input range first (`plotReferenceData :620-626`, "don't plot points that are out of bounds"); `plotChildData` (`:762-786`) applies no such filter, so a point past `input.maxX` is drawn ON the right frame line at its true y, against the reference value at `maxX`.
- **Why it fires routinely.** Chart selection is by age TODAY: `childAgeInMonths < graduatingAgeInMonth` with `graduatingAgeInMonth = 26` (`Pages/WellChild/ProgressReport/View.elm:1323-1339`, `Backend/Person/Utils.elm:260` — "children graduate from all groups at the age of 26 month"), while the 0–2 configs stop at `maxX = 365 * 2` days (`View.elm:194, :281, :455`). Every measurement taken after the second birthday of a child still under 26 months has `diffDays birthDate dateMeasured > 730` and is pinned to the 2-year edge. From the shipped tables (girls, day 788 vs 730 LMS): a child at exactly −2.0 z at 25.9 months is drawn where the 24-month reference puts **−1.77 z (length) / −1.71 z (weight)** — inside the green band, above the −2 line. Head-circumference 0–2 has the same clamp.
- **Same shape at the other edges.** On the 5–19 / 5–10 charts (children ≥ 60 months) every pre-5-year measurement has `Months m < 61` and stacks on the left frame line (`heightForAgeConfig5To19 :252 minX = 61`); the weight-for-age 5–10 chart (`:339 maxX = 120`) pins all measurements of 10–13-year-olds (encounters are open to anyone under 13, `Pages/IndividualEncounterParticipants/View.elm:108-119`) on the 10-year edge; y-overflow is pinned likewise (a 44 cm birth length on the 0–5 chart, `minY = 45`, lands in the orange band rather than red).
- **Live scale (ihangane, 2026-09-03):** **5,981 children / 7,296 weight measurements** taken between day 731 and 790 of life (drawn pinned on the 0–2 charts while the child is < 26 months); 44 children / 89 measurements past 10 years (pinned on the 5–10 chart).
- **Reachability / impact.** As B-314 (every child progress report). Clinician-facing misplacement of a child's most recent growth points at the moment of graduation; visual understatement of stunting/underweight by ~0.25 z. No site split.
- **Adjacent, spec-dependent (NOT part of the claim):** the chart picks the length-vs-height WHO table by age today (`weightForLength` for all points when < 26 months; `weightForHeight` for ALL points incl. under-2 ones when 26–60 months — `View.elm:989-1037`, `ProgressReport/View.elm:1351-1371`) while the verdict picks it per measurement at 731 days (`Measurement/View.elm:230-236`); the tables differ by the 0.7 cm lying/standing adjustment (≈0.18 z at 75 cm boys). A single 0–5 WFH chart makes some disagreement inherent — decide with the spec.
