### B-574 — Prenatal (CHW): a postpartum visit left unfinished on an earlier day locks the woman's Antenatal page — First, Subsequent and Postpartum buttons are all off, the open visit cannot be re-entered, and the CHW cannot record her postpartum visit — READY · MED · tier 3 · CONFIRMED by reading (orchestrator read all four anchors on e7f1e46e1; not run, not sized) · DEPLOYED, both sites, CHW only · B-205's consequence nobody recorded · R54 unit 5
- **Mechanism.** A CHW visit can be resumed only on the day it was started, and with no resumable visit every button is decided from the TYPE of the newest CHW visit alone, ended or not:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Participant/View.elm:345-350, 366-388 (viewActionsForChw)
                            activeEncounterId =
                                if isJust encounter.endDate || encounter.startDate /= currentDate then

      ...
                    ChwFirstEncounter ->
                        isNothing lastEncounterType
                    ChwSecondEncounter ->
                        lastEncounterType == Just ChwFirstEncounter && not encounterWasCompletedToday
                    ChwThirdPlusEncounter ->
                        (lastEncounterType == Just ChwSecondEncounter || lastEncounterType == Just ChwThirdPlusEncounter)
                            && not encounterWasCompletedToday
                    ChwPostpartumEncounter ->
                        case lastEncounterType of
                            Just lastEncounterType_ ->
                                (lastEncounterType_ /= ChwPostpartumEncounter)
                                    && not encounterWasCompletedToday
  ```
  With the newest CHW visit a `ChwPostpartumEncounter` started on an earlier day: First is off (a visit exists), the Subsequent button resolves to `ChwThirdPlusEncounter` (`:440-451`, the `_` branch) which needs a second / third visit last, Postpartum is off because the last visit is postpartum.
- **Why that state is always an abandoned visit.** A CHW postpartum visit expects Pregnancy Outcome (`Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:292-295`), completed only by `isJust assembled.participant.dateConcluded` (`:359-360`); End Encounter exempts only Photo and Ultrasound (`Pages/Prenatal/Encounter/View.elm:500-511`), and the emergency-referral bypass reads nurse diagnoses a CHW visit never has. So an ACTIVE pregnancy whose newest CHW visit is postpartum is a visit left before the outcome was saved.
- **Reachability.** CHW → Antenatal → woman → Postpartum → leaves before saving Pregnancy Outcome (back arrow, another patient, tablet off overnight) → returns on a later day. Also from the Case Management Antenatal dialog's Postpartum start (`Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Update.elm:65-80`). No flag, no site split.
- **Ways out, all checked.** "Record Pregnancy Outcome" on the participant page needs a COMPLETED visit (`Participant/View.elm:118-119`): with one, the CHW can close the pregnancy there, and Postpartum then opens a second pregnancy that asks the outcome again (B-466 / B-373's shape); when the postpartum visit is the woman's only visit (the "no encounters → allow postpartum" branch) that button is off too. Case Management lists nothing. Otherwise the lock lasts until the pregnancy expires (EDD + 92 days; about 372 days from registration with no EDD, `Participant/Utils.elm:69-79`). The nurse flow is not affected — `postpartumEncounterInProcess` reopens an open nurse postpartum visit on any day.
- **Impact.** The mother's and newborn's postpartum danger signs, the outcome and the newborn link are not recorded by the CHW, or land in a duplicate pregnancy; the abandoned visit stays open for good.
