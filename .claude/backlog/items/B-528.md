### B-528 — Case Management and Stock Management caches: a downloaded follow-up, FBF or stock update from ANOTHER health centre is folded into the logged-in health centre's cache, while a fresh fetch is shard-filtered — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED (code + scout's scratch elm-test; reach needs a device syncing more than one HC) · DEPLOYED · R48 unit 3
- **Mechanism.** Every follow-up-bearing revision branch maps the cache of the *logged-in* HC, never the revision's own:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/Update.elm:5372-5378 (AcuteIllnessFollowUpRevision; same shape at :5573, :6117, :6368, :7858 and the other follow-up / labs / FBF / stock-update branches — 12 `mapFollowUpMeasurements` call sites)
          AcuteIllnessFollowUpRevision uuid data ->
              let
                  modelWithMappedFollowUp =
                      mapFollowUpMeasurements
                          healthCenterId
                          (\measurements -> { measurements | acuteIllness = measurementActionConsideringDeletedField uuid data measurements.acuteIllness })
  ```
  `data.healthCenter` / the row's shard is not compared. The fetch that builds the same cache is shard-filtered: `nodes.js:654-658` `viewFollowUpMeasurements` keeps `item.shard === shard`; `viewStockManagementMeasurements` / `viewVillageStockManagementMeasurements` (`:805`, `:859`) likewise. `ChildFbf`/`MotherFbf`/`StockUpdate` branches do the same into `stockManagementMeasurements[healthCenterId]` and mark `stockManagementData` for recomputation.
- **Reachability.** A device that syncs more than one authority (Device page "Start syncing" an extra HC, `Pages/Device/View.elm:231`; 78 of 1,086 nurses on the Rwanda copy are assigned to more than one HC) downloads HC B's rows while Case Management or Stock Management for HC A is open or was open in the last five minutes (the `forget` cycle, `App/Update.elm:1465-1475`).
- **Impact.** Case Management for A lists B's follow-ups; Stock Management for A recomputes its month figures with B's FBF distributions and stock updates until the cache is forgotten. How many devices sync two authorities is not measurable from the server (sync info lives on the device). Both sites.
- **Evidence.** Traced by scout and orchestrator; scout's scratch test: an `AcuteIllnessFollowUp` with `healthCenter = Just hcB` folded under `healthCenterId = Just hcA` lands in `followUpMeasurements[hcA]`.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Guard the follow-up / FBF / stock folds with `data.healthCenter == healthCenterId` (or map the cache keyed by the row's own HC). Sibling of B-529 (same audit).
