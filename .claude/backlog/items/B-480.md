### B-480 — Statistical Queries › Prenatal (ANC): the outcomes and delivery-location tables count a delivery from before the FROM date when a postpartum visit falls in the range, so Peripartum and ANC disagree on deliveries for the same dates — READY · VERIFIED (code read; raised as finding 1 in review round 1 of PR #2355) · tier 4 · DEPLOYED
- **Found 2026-10-14** in review round 1 of #2355 (B-447), finding 1. Not fixed there: the ANC report reads a cohort (pregnancies with a visit in the range), a different question from Peripartum's deliveries-in-range.
- **Mechanism.** The shared pre-filter keeps a pregnancy with any visit in the range and blanks only a `dateConcluded` after TO. The ANC report then calls it completed when `dateConcluded` is set, and reads outcomes and delivery locations from `completed`:
```elm
-- server/elm/src/Pages/Reports/View.elm:1460-1466
        ( completed, active ) =
            List.partition
                (\participantData ->
                    -- Pregnancy is considered completed if
                    -- either conclusion date was set.
                    isJust participantData.dateConcluded
                        || ...
```
  A delivery on 20 Mar with a postpartum visit on 5 Apr counts in the Q1 and Q2 outcome tables. After #2355, Peripartum counts it in Q1 only.
- **Open question (read the ANC report's spec issue before building).** Is the outcomes table meant as "outcomes of the pregnancies followed in the range" (then the before-FROM case is arguably correct) or "deliveries in the range" (then it should match Peripartum)?
- **Live sizing.** Q2 2026 on ihangane: 25 deliveries dated before FROM sit on pregnancies with an in-range visit (the B-447 measurement); how many of them reach the ANC tables is not measured.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Bound outcomes by `dateConcluded` within FROM–TO in the two tables, if the spec says deliveries in range.
