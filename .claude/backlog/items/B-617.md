### B-617 — Completion report immunisations: two doses of one vaccine entered with the same date lose the last dose, so the next dose expected is the wrong one — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (review round 2 on #2564, finding 3; the B-584 blind test author hit it building fixtures) · not sized · side-finding of B-584
- **Mechanism.** Doses and dates are de-duplicated as two separate lists, then cut to the shorter length:
  ```php
  // server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_reports/hedley_reports.module (hedley_reports_generate_vaccination_progress_data, ≈4971)
  // array_unique on the doses and on the dates separately, each sorted, then both sliced to min(count)
  ```
- **Example.** Tetanus dose-1 2023-06-01, dose-2 and dose-3 both 2023-06-29 → dates [06-01, 06-29] → doses cut to [dose-1, dose-2] → the report treats dose-2 as last and expects dose 3 at 6 months instead of dose 4 at 12.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Build the dose → date map from each measurement's own dose and date pairs before removing repeats.
- 📊 **Sized live 2026-11-26 (ihangane).** No single record holds two doses on one date. Across records of the same bundle: **214 person / vaccine / date groups with two or more dose numbers, 139 persons**. Cross-programme pairs (Well Child + Child Scoreboard) not counted, so this is a floor.
- 🔎 **Samples 2026-11-26 (user: "how can 2 vaccine doses be given on a single date? Clinically, that does not make any sense").** Six cases read: each is a LATER dose saved at a later visit in its own record, carrying the PREVIOUS dose's date (person 5463338 OPV: dose-2 dated 2026-02-20 saved 02-28; dose-3 record 5533469 saved 2026-04-24, also dated 2026-02-20; same shape for 5396990 DTP/PCV13, 5533368, 5562476, 5566395). So the stored date is wrong, not the report. Source not found: the previous-dose picker in `Measurement/Utils.elm` starts at last dose + interval. Report fix on hold; the date's origin is the question.
- 🔬 **Investigation 2026-11-26 (user: "run B-617 investigation") — cause is B-292, already fixed.** Every sampled later dose is `administered-previously`, saved in one revision with the previous dose's date. Before #2349 (v1.18.3, 2026-10-25) `mergeVaccinationProgressDicts` reversed the dose order for EVERY Well Child child: the Child Scoreboard dict carries every vaccine type, empty ones included, and the vendored `Dict.merge` folds the left list from the right (`elm repl`: merging `[dose-1, dose-2]` with an empty dict gives `[dose-2, dose-1]`). So `lastDoseData` was dose 1, and the "given previously" picker started at dose 1 + interval, offering dose 2's date for dose 3. Live (ihangane): 214 groups / 139 children, 119 groups also Child Scoreboard, 95 Well Child only; newest 2026-09-15; **none after 2026-10-25**. The stored dates cannot be repaired: the real date is unknown.
