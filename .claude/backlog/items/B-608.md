### B-608 — Prenatal: diabetes reported to a visit from lab history is diagnosed again at the next visit, with a second hospital referral — READY · LOW · **tier 1 (standing rule: diagnosis determination)** ⚠ user to confirm · CONFIRMED by reading (orchestrator read the three anchors on develop `1197f3652`; not run) · DEPLOYED · not sized · side-finding of B-593 (review round 1, finding 3)
- **Mechanism.** Diabetes found from a result entered through lab history at visit C is stored only in C's `pastDiagnoses`. Both diabetes diagnosis rules look for an earlier diabetes in earlier visits' `diagnoses` only:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:2940-2952 (matchLabResultsAndExaminationPrenatalDiagnosis)
          Backend.PrenatalEncounter.Types.DiagnosisDiabetesInitialPhase ->
              (not <| diagnosedPreviouslyAnyOf diabetesDiagnoses assembled)
                  && resolveEGAWeeksAndThen
  ...
          Backend.PrenatalEncounter.Types.DiagnosisDiabetesRecurrentPhase ->
              (not <| diagnosedAtInitialPhase Backend.PrenatalEncounter.Types.DiagnosisDiabetesInitialPhase)
                  && (not <| diagnosedPreviouslyAnyOf diabetesDiagnoses assembled)
  ```
  The blood sugar test is withheld only when today's visit holds the reported diabetes:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:5570-5572 (expectLaboratoryTask)
                  TaskRandomBloodSugarTest ->
                      List.all (\diagnosis -> not <| EverySet.member diagnosis assembled.encounter.pastDiagnoses)
                          diabetesDiagnoses
  ```
  So the next visit D offers the test again. A diabetic result there diagnoses diabetes as new, and D refers her to hospital a second time. The report-to-origin guard in `Backend.Update` (`knownAtOrigin`, which reads `diagnosisKnownAnyOf`) exists to prevent exactly that second referral. Its comment: "when it's first diagnosed, the patient is referred to hospital. On next occasions, no next steps are required."
- **Reachability.** A: blood sugar sent to the lab. C: the result is entered from lab history → diabetes reported to C, hospital referral. D (20 weeks or earlier for the diabetes rule): blood sugar offered, diabetic → diabetes again → second referral.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Read `diagnosedOrReportedPreviouslyAnyOf diabetesDiagnoses` (added by B-593, PR #2543) in both diabetes rules, and in the test offer for earlier visits too. Check gestational diabetes (over 20 weeks) the same way.
