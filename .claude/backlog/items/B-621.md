### B-621 — Group-session photo pages: a sync stage held there keeps sending its request every tick (a finished general download, a failing photo upload) — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (reviews on #2565, round 1 finding 1 and round 2 findings 3 and 6) · not sized · side-finding of B-610
- **Mechanism.** `determineSyncStatus` holds the stage on the photo pages, but `BackendFetchMain` still dispatches that stage's work every tick:
  - opened during `SyncDownloadGeneral`, `BackendGeneralFetch` re-requests `/api/sync` after every reply, empty or not (`SyncManager/Update.elm`, `BackendGeneralFetch`);
  - opened during `SyncUploadPhoto`, a failing upload is retried every tick at the normal cycle speed — `getSyncSpeedForSubscriptions` gives `SyncUploadPhoto` the `_ -> syncCycle` branch, and the held status never reaches the error threshold or a `FileUploadIncident`.
- **Not reached through #2565:** after it, a cycle on these pages rests at `SyncIdle`; only a page opened mid-download or mid-photo-upload gets here.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Have `BackendFetchMain` skip a held stage, or give held stages the idle speed.
- **Seen in QA of #2565 (2026-11-19), the health-centre download too.** A child's page opened while `GET /api/sync/<hc uuid>` was in flight re-sent it about 20 times a second for the whole visit: 396 requests in 20 s on develop, 398 on the PR.
