### B-645 — Completion report › Nutrition Group: mothers at ACHI groups are expected to do Family Planning and Lactation, which the app never shows there — READY · CONFIRMED by reading (both rules read 2026-11-24 while building B-643) · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-643 (blind-test author + review round 1, PR #2586) · historical only
- **What is wrong.** `hedley_reports_nutrition_group_mother_expected_activities()` expects `family_planning` for every mother, and `lactation` (plus `mother_fbf` when breastfeeding) when `hedley_reports_fbf_activities_shown()` is true, which includes `achi`. The app has shown no mother activities at ACHI since ACHI was added (`Activity/Utils.elm` `getAllMotherActivities`: `Achi -> []`, commit `08a9c28b5`, 2020-11-25).
- **Impact.** ACHI mothers count Family Planning and Lactation as missed. ihangane: 3,488 ACHI attendances, 2020-12-14 → 2024-11-26, none since; FBF 128,796. vhw: none.
- **Fix shape (guess).** No mother activities expected at ACHI groups; regenerate Nutrition Group completion data.
