### B-051 — PIN cached before validation + non-logout session end leaves the previous nurse's HC/village selection to be silently inherited by the next nurse — READY · CONFIRMED · LOW-MED
- `App/Update.elm:1333-1352` `TryPinCode`: `Cmd.batch [checkPinCode, cachePinCode code]` writes the localStorage PIN BEFORE the check resolves (a wrong PIN is cached and re-tried every boot). The only clearer of pin/HC/village caches is the explicit Logout (:1065-1071); `SetLoggedIn (Failure _)` clears nothing; boot restores healthCenterId/villageId unconditionally from flags. Nurse A leaves without Sign Out → next boot auto-TryPinCode fails but A's village/HC remain → Nurse B logs in and lands on the menu with A's village pre-selected (if B is also authorized for it) → B records a day under the wrong village, never shown the selector.
- Fix: move `cachePinCode` into the `SetLoggedIn Success` branch; on Success compare nurse uuid vs a cached `lastNurseUuid` and `SetHealthCenter/Village Nothing` on mismatch. Severity LOW-MED (needs non-logout end + overlapping assignment).
