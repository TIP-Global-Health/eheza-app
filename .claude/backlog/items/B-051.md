### B-051 — PIN cached before validation + non-logout session end leaves the previous nurse's HC/village selection to be silently inherited by the next nurse — READY · CONFIRMED · LOW-MED
- `App/Update.elm:1333-1352` `TryPinCode`: `Cmd.batch [checkPinCode, cachePinCode code]` writes the localStorage PIN BEFORE the check resolves (a wrong PIN is cached and re-tried every boot). The only clearer of pin/HC/village caches is the explicit Logout (:1065-1071); `SetLoggedIn (Failure _)` clears nothing; boot restores healthCenterId/villageId unconditionally from flags. Nurse A leaves without Sign Out → next boot auto-TryPinCode fails but A's village/HC remain → Nurse B logs in and lands on the menu with A's village pre-selected (if B is also authorized for it) → B records a day under the wrong village, never shown the selector.
- Fix: move `cachePinCode` into the `SetLoggedIn Success` branch; on Success compare nurse uuid vs a cached `lastNurseUuid` and `SetHealthCenter/Village Nothing` on mismatch. Severity LOW-MED (needs non-logout end + overlapping assignment).
- **AMENDED R32 unit 6 (2026-09-15) — the inherited location can cross roles.** `nurseAuthorizedForLocation` checks only the health centre for a non-CHW (`client/src/elm/Backend/Nurse/Utils.elm:60-70`), while Case Management picks its CHW or nurse screen by whether a village is set, not by role:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/View.elm:90-96 (Fetch.elm:25-31 the same split)
          content =
              Maybe.map
                  (\villageId ->
                      viewWebData language (viewContentForChw language currentDate features villageId model db) identity followUps
                  )
                  mVillageId
  ```
  So after a CHW's session ends without Sign Out (or a CHW's role is changed while logged in — `UpdateNurseData` keeps the village), a nurse or lab tech of the parent HC lands on the menu with the CHW's village kept: the nurse's Case Management shows the CHW follow-up panes (Contact Tracing, Prenatal Labs and NCD Labs vanish), and a lab tech's only workflow (Prenatal Labs) is unreachable until Sign Out. Stock Management's context also keys on the village (`Pages/StockManagement/Model.elm:16-23`), inert today (`stock_management_hc` off on all four sites). Orchestrator read the view/fetch split and the gate. This fix's clear-on-different-nurse also removes it; a narrower option is branching on `isChw` in Case Management and Stock Management.
