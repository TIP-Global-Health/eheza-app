### B-529 — Revision fold misses a derived cache, ×3: (a) a positive HIV test or the first real HIV follow-up does not update the Case Management HIV pane's dummy entries; (b) a newly registered person is not added to `peopleInVillage`; (c) a downloaded resilience survey creates a partial `Success` list that blocks the real fetch — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED (code + scout's scratch elm-test ×4) · DEPLOYED · R48 unit 3
- **(a) HIV pane.** A fresh fetch synthesises a dummy `hiv_follow_up` for every person with a positive `prenatal_hiv_test` / `ncd_hiv_test` and no real `hiv_follow_up` (`client/src/js/nodes.js:703-770`). The fold has no counterpart: `PrenatalHIVTestRevision` (`Backend/Update.elm:7385-7400`) and `NCDHIVTestRevision` (`:6550`) touch only the encounter slot, so a newly positive patient is missing from the pane until the cache is forgotten; `HIVFollowUpRevision` (`:6117-6123`) inserts the real follow-up beside the dummy, so `generateHIVFollowUps` (`Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Utils.elm:456-503`, keys `(Nothing, person)` vs `(Just participant, person)`) lists the person twice. Small today (49 positive tests, 2 real follow-ups on the Rwanda copy). Related, not the same: B-160 (what the dummy suppression should mean), B-434 (tap creates a second HIV participant).
- **(b) Village residents.**
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/Update.elm:7123-7134
          PersonRevision uuid data ->
              ...
              ( { model
                  | personSearchesByName = Dict.empty
                  , personSearchesByNationalId = Dict.empty
                  , people = people
                }
  ```
  `peopleInVillage` (fetched by geo fields, `:1639-1662`) is neither updated nor dropped. Wanted by `Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Fetch.elm` (CHW: `resolveVillageResidents` filters every follow-up to residents), `Pages/EducationSession/Fetch.elm` (participant picker) and `Pages/StockManagement/Fetch.elm`. A CHW who visits one of these pages, registers a person, and returns within five minutes sees the stale list: the new person's follow-ups hidden, the education picker unable to select them. Different mechanism from B-352 (multi-word geo names break the fetch itself).
- **(c) Resilience surveys.**
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/Update.elm:7760-7770
                          |> Maybe.withDefault (Dict.insert data.nurse (Success (Dict.singleton uuid data)) model.resilienceSurveysByNurse)
  ```
  The only `handleRevision` branch that creates a parent from one revision. `resilience_survey` is a general (unsharded) entity, so every device folds every nurse's surveys; `Backend/Fetch.elm:513-516` then says the list is loaded and the key is never in `dataWanted`, so it is never forgotten. The Messaging Center (`Pages/MessagingCenter/View.elm:69-100`, `Utils.elm:139`) may offer a survey already completed and score against an incomplete history for one visit. 87 surveys on the Rwanda copy.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** (a) mark `followUpMeasurements[hc]` `NotAsked` on HIV-test and HIV-follow-up revisions, or mirror the SW synthesis; (b) drop `peopleInVillage` on `PersonRevision` (all, or the matching village via `getVillageIdByGeoFields`); (c) update an existing `Success` entry only, like every other by-person cache. Sibling of B-528.
