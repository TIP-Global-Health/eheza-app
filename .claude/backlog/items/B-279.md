### B-279 — Well Child head circumference "not taken" checkbox toggles the raw form, so after a reload the first click on a saved not-taken record does nothing — READY · CONFIRMED (anchors verified) · LOW · tier 4 · R26 unit 2 · B-274 shape
- **Mechanism.** `ToggleHeadCircumferenceNotTaken` (`Pages/WellChild/Activity/Update.elm:509-535`) reads `model.nutritionAssessmentData.headCircumferenceForm` raw and computes `notTaken = Maybe.map not form.measurementNotTaken |> Maybe.withDefault True`. The View draws the checkbox from the resolved form (`View.elm:812-814` → `headCircumferenceFormWithDefault`, `Utils.elm:722`: `measurementNotTaken = or form.measurementNotTaken (member NoteNotTaken notes)`), so a saved not-taken record renders checked; after a reload `wellChildActivityPages` is fresh (`App/Model.elm:325`, in-memory) so the raw field is `Nothing` and the toggle computes `True` — re-checking it. The second click unchecks. `SetHeightNotTaken`/`SetWeightNotTaken` (`:428, :708`) are immune because the widget passes an explicit `Bool`; `resolveFormWithDefaults` sits unused in the same `let` (`:38-47`).
- **Reachability.** Nutrition Assessment → Head Circumference saved as not taken → reload or re-login → reopen → click the checkbox to enter a value.
- **Impact.** Both sites. A second click recovers; nothing wrong is stored.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Resolve the form through `resolveFormWithDefaults .headCircumference headCircumferenceFormWithDefault` before toggling, or pass the explicit `Bool` as the height/weight handlers do.
