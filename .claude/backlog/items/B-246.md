### B-246 — Completion report: its query filters on publication status instead of the soft-delete flag, so encounters an administrator deleted are still counted — READY · CONFIRMED (both queries + soft-delete writer verified) · MED · tier 3 · R25 unit 7
- ⚠ **Gated with the rest of the Completion-report batch** (B-179, B-180, B-181, B-183, B-186, B-187) on the one unanswered question: **is the Completion report actually used?** Do not present as ready to build until that is answered.
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_reports_generate_completion_data_for_restful` (`hedley_reports.module:3312-3320`) filters `->condition('fr.deleted', 0)` and `->condition('n.status', NODE_PUBLISHED)`. `fr.deleted` is Field API's *deleted field instance* column — unrelated to the app's soft delete, which sets `field_deleted = 1` and leaves `status` untouched (`hedley_admin_mark_as_deleted_action`, `hedley_admin.module:33-48`, verified: it sets the field and saves, nothing else; the cascade in `hedley_person_process_deleted_update` behaves the same).
- **The sibling 1,400 lines above gets it right and says so.** `hedley_reports_generate_results_data_for_restful` (`:1885-1892`) adds `leftJoin('field_data_field_deleted', 'fd', …)` plus `db_or()->isNull('fd.field_deleted_value')->condition('fd.field_deleted_value', 0)` under the comment *"Replicates the exclude_deleted query tag"*. `hedley_ncda_generate_results_data_for_restful` has it too. The completion variant is the copy that was not updated.
- **Why the stale blobs persist.** The *generation* queries do exclude deleted nodes, so a blob written before deletion is never revised — it simply keeps being served.
- **Reachability.** `POST /api/reports-data` with `app_type=completion` → `HedleyRestfulReportsData::getData` → this function → `admin/reports/completion*`.
- **Impact.** Completion percentages and encounter counts include encounters marked erroneous. A concrete recurring producer: patient merges soft-delete the duplicate's `individual_participant` nodes (`hedley_patient.drush.inc:844, 1266, 1440`) and the cascade carries that to their encounters — so after every merge the duplicate's encounters are still counted beside the originals. All sites.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Add the same `leftJoin` + `db_or()` pair to the completion closure, verbatim from the results sibling.
