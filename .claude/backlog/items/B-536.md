### B-536 — Individual encounters, read side: every reader of a person's participant takes ONE participant of the type (`List.head` on the by-person dict, `range(0, 1)` on the server), so a child with duplicate participants has a split history that no device can see whole — fold ALL participants of the type instead — READY · MED · tier 1 (umbrella fix for B-531's assessment determination; also delivers B-532) · CONFIRMED (code; data in B-531/B-532/B-195) · DEPLOYED (both sites) · spun out of the B-195 two-layer plan, 2026-11-02 — layer 1 of 2
- **Why this exists as its own item.** B-195 (PARKED) is the writer race; the writer guard shipped in v1.18.0/v1.18.1 and the residual is ≈ 1 event per 2 months, all multi-device (see B-195, 2026-11-02 section). What still hurts is the READ side: ≈ 2,500 accumulated duplicates (nutrition 859 children, child-scoreboard 203, well-child 57, home-visit/ncd/hiv/tb smaller) and readers that pick one. Once every reader folds all participants, a duplicate costs nothing clinically and the repair (B-537) becomes cleanliness, not safety.
- **The pick.**
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/Utils.elm:318-321
  resolveIndividualParticipantForPerson personId encounterType db =
      resolveIndividualParticipantsForPerson personId encounterType db
          |> List.head
  ```
  The by-person dict is built from the SW query in uuid order (`nodes.js:1073-1131`, no sort), so the head is fixed but arbitrary and does not prefer the participant of the encounter being viewed.
