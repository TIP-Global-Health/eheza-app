### B-612 — Dashboard: diabetes or gestational diabetes found from a lab-history result is never counted in the prenatal diabetes figures — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (reviewer's claim, anchors checked; not run) · not sized · side-finding of B-608 (review round 2 on #2551, finding 6)
- **Mechanism.** A result entered from lab history stores diabetes in the reporting visit's `pastDiagnoses`. The client dashboard's diabetes figures read encounter `diagnoses` only:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Dashboard/View.elm:1361-1363
          newlyDiagnosesGestationalDiabetesPregnancies =
              ...
                  [ DiagnosisGestationalDiabetesInitialPhase, DiagnosisGestationalDiabetesRecurrentPhase ]
  ```
  `countTotalNumberOfPatientsWithGestationalDiabetes` (`Pages/Dashboard/Utils.elm:1759`) reads the same set. Before #2551 such a pregnancy was counted only when a later visit re-diagnosed it (the B-608 defect).
- **Impact.** Undercount of diabetes pregnancies on the nurse dashboard; B-593's sizing found one lab-history diabetes report ever on ihangane. The server Statistical Queries reportedly read past diagnoses (not checked here).
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Count `pastDiagnoses` diabetes in both dashboard readers, once per pregnancy.
