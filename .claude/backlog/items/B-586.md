Clinically, gestational hypertension is on the preeclampsia spectrum rather than a pre-existing risk factor — the reason the program may or may not want it counted; the code's inclusion may be deliberate, nothing in the issue trail says so.
- **Diabetes, same question, not asserted.** The spec's diabetes line has no "chronic" word ("Diabetes Mellitus" or "a diagnosis from lab results"), so gestational diabetes counting is consistent with it; mention when asking.
