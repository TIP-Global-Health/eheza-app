### B-546 — Case Management Nutrition pane: a follow-up the CHW never answered with a home visit stays listed for years, and a later nutrition visit that finds the child healthy cannot clear it, so ≈ 4,900 children (≈ 1,950 of them now five or older) sit on CHWs' lists with a years-old assessment and a tap opens a Home Visit on them — READY · MED-LOW · tier 3 · CONFIRMED (code; sized on the 2026-11-02 ihangane copy) · DEPLOYED · B-492 / B-374's shape on the third pane · R49 unit 3
