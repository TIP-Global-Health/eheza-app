### B-619 — Completion report (Prenatal): a tetanus dose the app offers at once because the last dose was before this pregnancy is not expected until the interval passes — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading the report side (review round 1 on #2564, finding 1); app rule not re-read · side-finding of B-584
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_reports_prenatal_add_immunisation_activities()` waits only for the dose interval (`+4 weeks` / `+6 months` / `+12 months`). The app (`nextVaccinationDataForVaccine`, `Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm`) offers the next dose immediately when the weeks since the last dose exceed the EGA in weeks, i.e. the last dose was before this pregnancy.
- **Example.** Dose 3 on 2024-06-01; new pregnancy, first nurse visit 2025-02-10: the app asks for dose 4 that day; the report expects it from 2025-06-01, so a skipped dose 4 is not counted.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Mirror the app's "before this pregnancy" rule in the report.
