### B-620 — Photo measurements: a late-synced or edited OLDER photo measurement replaces the person's newer profile photo — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (reviews on #2565, round 1 finding 4 and round 2 finding 4) · not sized · side-finding of B-610
- **Mechanism.** Every create and PATCH of a group session, Nutrition, Well Child or Family Nutrition photo copies its photo onto the person, however old the measurement is:
  ```php
  // server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_restful/hedley_restful.module (hedley_restful_copy_photo_to_person, after #2565)
  try {
    $person_wrapper->field_photo->set($photo);
    $person_wrapper->save();
  }
  ```
  A PATCH keeps the stored image (the client drops the `photo` key for a backend photo), so editing last month's measurement copies last month's photo back over this month's.
