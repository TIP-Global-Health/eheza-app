### B-646 — Person registration from a group session: when the expected age is "adult or child", or the person has no relation, the birth-date picker runs back 120 years, so a child too old for an FBF or PMTCT group can be saved and is then not listed for it — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (review round 2 of PR #2592) · DEPLOYED · side-finding of B-560
- **Mechanism.** In `Pages/Person/View.elm` (`GroupEncounterOrigin`), only `CreatePerson (Just _)` with `ExpectChild` uses `earliestGroupChildBirthDate`; `ExpectAdultOrChild` and every other operation take `Date.add Years -120 currentDate`. The add-to-group list then applies `groupTakesChild` (26 months for FBF / PMTCT).
- **Reachability.** Group session → register a person beside a related person whose age is unknown, pick a birth date 3 years ago, save: the child is not offered for adding to the group. Not sized.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Apply the group's earliest date whenever the entered birth date makes the person a child, or confirm this path cannot create a child.
- 📊 **Sized live 2026-11-26 (ihangane; vhw and uvl 0).** The child is not refused: the relationship page then enrols them in the session's group with no age check, ending the enrolment at birth + 26 months, already past.
