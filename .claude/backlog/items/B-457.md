### B-457 — Admin Elm app (`server/elm`): no unit tests can be written, because it has no elm-test setup, so pure helpers such as the CSV writer's `csvRow` have no test — READY · VERIFIED (no `tests/` folder, no test step in CI) · LOW · tier 4
- **Found 2026-10-07** in the review of #2312 (B-446), round 1 (finding 1).
- **Mechanism.** `server/elm` has `src/` and `review/` only; CI runs elm-review and the bundle check on it, never tests. The client's e2e `reporting.spec.ts` checks the Download CSV button exists, not the file.
- **Impact.** Report logic (bucketing, CSV, date ranges) can regress unnoticed. Every server-Elm fix so far shipped without a pinned test (B-245 used a PHP-side guard instead).
- **Fix shape (guess).** A `server/elm/tests/` with elm-test pinned to the project-local binary, a CI step beside `lint_elm_review`, and `csvRow` as the first test. Weigh CI time first.
