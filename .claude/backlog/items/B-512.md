### B-512 — Nutrition assessment: a "malnutrition-signs" assessment is stored with "none" as its only sign, so the screens show "Malnutrition Signs: None of these" — READY · CONFIRMED in data, writer NOT traced · LOW · tier 4 · side finding of B-322 (PR #2426, review round 2 finding 3)
- **Found 2026-10-26** while checking PR #2426's round-2 finding 3 against the ihangane copy (`ddev-sec-ihangane-db`).
- **Data.** 46 of the 65 records whose `field_nutrition_assesment` is `malnutrition-signs` have no `field_nutrition_signs` value other than `none`: `well_child_follow_up` 14, `nutrition_follow_up` 13, `follow_up` 9, `nutrition_nutrition` 5, `well_child_nutrition` 5. Each holds that one assessment only. Created 2022-02 → 2026-02-27 (the `well_child_nutrition` ones are the newest).
- **Why it looks wrong.** The app only produces `AssesmentMalnutritionSigns` when a danger sign other than "none" was ticked:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Backend/NutritionEncounter/Utils.elm (generateNutritionAssessment)
                                if dangerSignsPresent then
                                    [ AssesmentMalnutritionSigns dangerSigns ]
  ```
  So something stored the assessment without its signs, or changed the signs after the assessment was saved. Not traced.
- **Impact.** Display: Case Management and the child progress report read these as "Malnutrition Signs: None of these". Whether any follow-up logic keys on the assessment is not checked.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Trace the writer first (the follow-up encoder takes `nutrition_signs` from the assessment's head only, `Backend/Measurement/Encoder.elm` ~:2381; the nutrition form may let signs change without recomputing the assessment).
