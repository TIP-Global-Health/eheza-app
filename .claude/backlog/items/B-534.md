### B-534 — Healthy Start gestational weight gain: the expected-gain window runs from the previous weight's SAVE date, not its visit date, so an earlier weight re-saved weeks later shrinks the window and biases the verdict toward "adequate" — READY · MED-LOW · tier 2 (B-235's surface, deployed; verdict is persisted and reported) · CONFIRMED (code; 380 affected next visits on ihangane) · DEPLOYED (Healthy Start sites — ihangane per the R27 flag snapshot) · R48 unit 1
- **Mechanism.** Every measurement save stamps `dateMeasured` with the device's current date, also on edits (`Backend/Utils.elm:259-283` `saveMeasurementCmd`, `patchFull`). The previous weight's date is taken from that stamp:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:4198-4213
  resolvePreviousValueWithDate assembled measurementFunc valueFunc =
      assembled.nursePreviousEncountersData
          |> List.filterMap
              (.measurements
                  >> measurementFunc
                  >> Maybe.map
                      (Tuple.second
                          >> (\measurement ->
                                  ( measurement.dateMeasured, valueFunc measurement.value )
                             )
                      )
              )
          |> List.reverse
          |> List.head
  ```
  `Pages/Prenatal/Activity/View.elm:3162` takes `weightPreviousValueWithDate = resolvePreviousValueWithDate assembled .nutrition .weight` and (`:3233-3252`) feeds its date to `resolveGWGClassificationForHealthyStart currentDate … previousWeightDate …` (`Utils.elm:1866-1910`), where `totalDays = Date.diff Days previousWeightDate currentDate` × the per-day standard = `expectedWeightGain`, compared with `currentWeight − previousWeight`. `nursePreviousEncountersData` carries the encounter's `startDate` beside the measurement, but the save stamp is used. The non-Healthy-Start branch `resolveGWGClassification` (`:1810`) takes no date and is unaffected.
- **Reachability.** The Prenatal nurse's participant page opens the latest nurse encounter whenever it has no `endDate`, with no day bound (`Pages/Prenatal/Participant/View.elm:207-221`) — a routine path: on ihangane ≈ 1.6 % of every non-lab prenatal bundle is dated more than a day after its encounter (`vitals` 1,079 of 54,605; `prenatal_nutrition` 810 of 54,554), 908 of the 1,079 late vitals exactly on the encounter's end day (nurse re-opens, edits, ends), ≈ 110 encounters a year. Re-saving the Nutrition task unchanged is enough to move the stamp. Also hit by a plain post-midnight save (37 rows).
- **Impact.** A woman who gained 1 kg over 6 weeks, whose earlier weight was re-saved 4 weeks after that visit, is assessed over a 2-week window and classified adequate. Shown on the form and persisted as `IndicatorAdequateGWG` / `IndicatorInadequateGWG` on the encounter (`Backend/PrenatalEncounter/Update.elm:55-72`), counted by the Healthy Start report (`hedley_reports.module:1007-1012`). Live: **380** `prenatal_nutrition` records re-saved > 1 day after their encounter with a later encounter of the same pregnancy — 380 next visits whose window started from an edit date; 741 of the 1,079 late vitals are > 30 days late, so the shrink is typically weeks.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Pair the previous weight with its encounter's `startDate` (already in the tuple) — rule: encounter date. One-line change in `resolvePreviousValueWithDate`; check its other callers want the same. Members of the same class at lower stakes are in B-535; readers that deliberately want the recording day (lab expiry, group completion start, surveys/stock) are listed there and must not change.
