### B-641 — Statistical Queries › ANC Contacts: the "attended ANC contact within 1st trimester" row leaves out every woman seen more than once by 12 weeks — READY · CONFIRMED by reading (orchestrator read the row, the counter, the label and the sibling report; nothing run, not sized) · LOW · tier 4 · R70 unit 2 · ⚠ spec question, the issue behind the row was not read
