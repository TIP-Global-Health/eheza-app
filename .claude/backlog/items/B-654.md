### B-654 — E2E date helpers: "one month ago" is still this month on seven days a year, so the reporting spec's backdated encounters miss the report column they are asserted in — READY · CONFIRMED by reading (orchestrator read the spec, the helper sites and the column builder; the scout ran the day-by-day arithmetic) · LOW · tier 4 · R75 unit 1 · tests only, no patient data, not sized
- **What is wrong.** The suite subtracts months with JavaScript's `setMonth`, which keeps the day of month. When the target month is shorter, the date rolls forward: on 31 December, "one month ago" is 31 November, which JavaScript reads as 1 December.
  ```ts
  -- client/e2e/reporting.spec.ts:1322-1331
      await step('Backdate nutrition encounters to previous month', async () => {
        const lastMonth = new Date();
        lastMonth.setMonth(lastMonth.getMonth() - 1);
        backdateNutritionEncounter(nutrChildName, lastMonth);
        backdateNutritionEncounter(hvChildName, lastMonth);
        ...
        backdateFamilyNutritionEncounter(familyMuacChildName, lastMonth);
      });
  ```
  The spec's own comment says why the month matters: "The nutrition report only shows completed months. Backdating makes our test data appear in the first (newest) column." The assertions then read `values[0]` of the prevalence table (`reporting.spec.ts:1792-1809`, scout's anchor) and expect Stunting Severe, Underweight Severe, SAM and MAM above zero.
- **Days affected (scout ran the arithmetic over 2027).** 29, 30 and 31 March; 31 May; 31 July; 31 October; 31 December. Next: **2026-12-31**.
- **Reachability.** The `e2e_reporting` CI job, on every build that runs CI, and any local run on those days.
- **Impact.** On those days the three backdated encounters stay in the current month, which the report does not show; the four assertions then depend on the demo data alone. A false red, never a false green. ⚠ **Not established:** whether the job really fails — nobody ran the suite on such a date or checked whether migrated demo data already gives non-zero figures for last month.
- **Same arithmetic elsewhere, consequences not traced.** Child birth dates: `helpers/common.ts:1153`, `helpers/well-child.ts:128`, `helpers/group-session.ts:368`, `helpers/family-nutrition.ts:145` and `:257` (`dob.setMonth(dob.getMonth() - ageMonths)`) — the child comes out one to three days younger than the whole months asked for on a few days a year. Most exposed, per the scout: the reporting spec's 11-month child asserted into the "10-19 mos" bucket, its 1-month newborn, the child scoreboard's 3-month child. Also `helpers/reports.ts:183` (`backdatePersonCreated`) and `helpers/well-child.ts:576` (pregnancy summary's date concluded) — found by the orchestrator's grep, not traced.
- **Already in the suite.** `child-birth-date-range.spec.ts:33-38` has an `addMonths` that clamps to the last day of the target month (scout's anchor).
- **Fix shape (guess).** One shared month-subtraction helper that clamps the day, used at all eight sites.
- **Related.** B-002 is the app-side `setMonth` item, a different site.
