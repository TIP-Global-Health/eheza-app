### B-219 — Patient consolidation: moved measurements/encounters/participants keep the DUPLICATE's shards, so transferred history can miss the original's group-clinic devices — CONFIRMED-structural (orchestrator-read `_associate_content_by_field` + `hedley_device_assign_shards` early-return + the relationship handler that already clears shards for this exact reason) · LOW-MED · tier 4 · both sites · R23 unit 6
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_patient.drush.inc:1502-1508` `_associate_content_by_field()` only sets the reference field and saves. `hedley_device_assign_shards()` (`hedley_device.module:226-230`) returns early when `field_shards` is already populated, and `hedley_patient_node_update` recomputes content shards only when the PERSON's shards change (the merge does not change them). The relationship (`:335-340`, with a comment explaining exactly this) and pmtct (`:~597`) handlers clear `field_shards` before saving; the measurement / encounter / individual_participant handlers do not — the un-updated siblings.
- **Impact.** Original and duplicate must share `field_health_center` (validate), so divergence comes only from group-clinic shards at OTHER health centres: if the original participates in a group at HC B and the duplicate did not, the transferred history never reaches HC B devices though the person does; the symmetric case sends content to devices lacking the person. Edge case; frequency unknown.
- **Fix shape (first guess):** clear `field_shards` in `_associate_content_by_field` for bundles carrying the field (guard with `field_info_instance`), or call `hedley_patient_recalculate_shards_for_person_content($original)` at the end of execute.
- **Tail note (low confidence, NOT an item — agent could not establish reachability):** `hedley_patient_node_insert` (`hedley_patient.module:70-86`) returns early on `isset($node->imported)`, and `hedley_person_create_basic_node()` (`hedley_person.module:~1012`) always sets `imported = 1` — but it is also used by the LIVE `hedley_chw_process_new_participation()` (`hedley_chw.module:~134/~210`), so a CHW-group participation created on normal enrolment never adds the CHW clinic's HC to the person's shards. Only matters when the village's CHW group belongs to a different HC than the person's `field_health_center`; unknown whether that ever happens. Check with a live count before promoting.
