### B-508 — Completion report: Acute Illness follow-ups expect a Malaria test after a positive one — ✅ IMPLEMENTED + MERGED 2026-10-26 (`0f6a3c590`), 2026-10-25 (issue #2418, PR #2419, with B-507) · CONFIRMED (code + live data) · LOW · tier 4
- **Mechanism.** The follow-up branch of the malaria rule reads the earlier result from `field_rapid_test_result`, which belongs to `covid_testing`. Malaria tests store it in `field_malaria_rapid_test` (as the module's other two reads do), so a positive is never found.

  ```php
  -- server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_reports/hedley_reports.module:3983 (before the fix)
          $malaria_rapid_test_result = $prev_measurements_by_type['malaria_testing']->field_rapid_test_result[LANGUAGE_NONE][0]['value'];
  ```

- **Live (ihangane, 2026-10-25).** 4,306 follow-ups come after a positive test in the same illness; 31 of them store Malaria testing as expected. Report-only.
- **Found** by B-507's harness: the CHW-only control still expected Malaria after a positive test. Fixed in its own commit on PR #2419; the harness shows the control flipping.
