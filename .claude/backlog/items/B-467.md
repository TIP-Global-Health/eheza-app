### B-467 — CHW Dashboard: a follow-up resolved on the first day after the selected month is left out of that month's Case Management cards, although it was open on the month's last day — READY · VERIFIED (code read; raised as finding 4 in the medium review of PR #2341) · LOW · tier 4 · DEPLOYED
- **Found 2026-10-13** in the review of #2341 (B-465), round 1, finding 4. Pre-existing: a past month's limit date was the 1st of the next month before #2341 too.
- **Mechanism.** The shared keep-rule is strict:
```elm
-- client/src/elm/Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/Utils.elm:477-483
filterResolvedFollowUps : NominalDate -> Maybe NominalDate -> Bool
filterResolvedFollowUps limitDate resolutionDate =
    Maybe.map
        (\date ->
            -- Keep the follow up only if it was resolved AFTER the limit
            -- date, meaning it was still open as of that date.
            Date.compare limitDate date == LT
```
  With August selected the limit is Sep 1. A follow-up resolved on Sep 1 was open on Aug 31, but is dropped from the August Home Visit, Acute Illness and ANC cards.
- **Why not `>=`.** The Case Management pane calls the generators with today as the limit (`Pages/GlobalCaseManagement/View.elm`, `viewContentForChw`), so `>=` would keep follow-ups resolved today in the pane; `filterResolvedFollowUpsTests` pin that they are dropped.
- **Impact.** One day's resolutions per past month on the dashboard only; nothing stored.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Have the dashboard compare against the selected date itself (resolved after the selected date), leaving the pane's rule alone; or have the pane pass tomorrow and switch the rule to `>=`.
