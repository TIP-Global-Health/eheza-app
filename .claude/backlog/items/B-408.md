### B-408 — Both deploy skills run their clean-tree and terminus preflight checks before the `ddev restart` that invalidates them — READY · CONFIRMED (scout traced the ordering and the hook; orchestrator confirmed the lock files are tracked) · LOW · tier 4 · R34 unit 4
- **Mechanism.** `deploy-release/SKILL.md` Step 1 checks `git status -s -uno` (item 2) and `terminus auth:whoami` (item 5); Step 2 item 2 then runs `ddev restart`. `deploy-multidev/SKILL.md` has the identical ordering (Step 1 items 2 and 4, Step 2 item 2). The restart fires `post-start: - exec-host: ddev client-install`, whose `npm install` (`.ddev/commands/web/client-install:60-63`) rewrites `client/package-lock.json` and `client/yarn.lock` — both **tracked**, even though `client/.gitignore` also lists `package-lock.json`. So the tree that was clean at preflight is dirty by the time `deployPantheon`'s own check runs:
  ```php
  -- server/RoboFile.php:40-48
      // (dirty-tree guard — aborts the deploy)
  ```
  and the deploy aborts. The terminus session likewise does not reliably survive the restart.
- **Impact.** Every deploy driven by either skill. This is a recorded, recurring failure whose durable remedy (`git update-index --skip-worktree` on the lock files) lives only in the memory note [[deploy-multidev-recurring-gotchas]] — while the *other* gotcha from that same note (orphaned-module `cc all` exit 137, `admin_views_system_display` / `hedley_pusher`) **has** been folded into `_deploy-common/deploy-reference.md:106` with its "verify the module is genuinely absent first" caveat. One of a pair was migrated and the other was not.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Re-run the two checks after the restart, and fold the skip-worktree remedy into the reference's troubleshooting list next to the existing exit-137 entry.
