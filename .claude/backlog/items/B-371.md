  `NextSteps` completes only when all its tasks do (`Utils.elm:354-355`), and End Encounter needs no mandatory activity pending, or Next Steps completed when an emergency referral is required (`Pages/Prenatal/Encounter/View.elm:~499-511` `viewActionButton`). `ChwSecondEncounter`/`ChwThirdPlusEncounter` offer only Danger Signs, Birth Plan (2nd), Health Education and Next Steps (`Activity/Utils.elm:~258-290`) — `globalLmpDate` comes only from a Pregnancy Dating or nurse ultrasound record (`Pages/Prenatal/Encounter/Utils.elm:243-258`).
- **Reachability.** CHW records visit 1 but leaves before Pregnancy Dating (or the CHW's first visit was never recorded); the next day the participant page offers visit 2 (active CHW encounter = started today only). From then on no CHW encounter of that pregnancy can end. Scout also traced two nurse branches: a first visit whose emergency diagnosis needs no EGA (e.g. eclampsia from convulsions) reaches Next Steps before Pregnancy Dating — End blocked until the nurse goes back and dates it; and later nurse visits after an older nurse encounter closed undated (12 such rows from 2025 on ihangane).
- **Evidence.** Orchestrator, vhw.live 2026-09-15, `chw-2`/`chw-3` encounters scheduled ≥ 2026-03-01, deleted excluded, "dated" = the participant has any non-deleted LMP record:

Scout: ihangane 7 undated CHW 2/3 visits, 5 open, none with a date; the 38 vhw visits belong to 30 women (15 with an abandoned open first visit, 15 with no first visit at all — a separate question).
- **Impact.** The encounter cannot be ended from the app, stays open for good and never stores its next visit; the CHW sees a Save that does nothing. Measurements already saved are kept. Both CHW sites (vhw heaviest).
- **Fix shape (guess).** Do not expect Next Visit when no date can be computed, or offer Pregnancy Dating on CHW visits 2/3 (and later nurse visits) while no LMP exists. Which one is a workflow question.
