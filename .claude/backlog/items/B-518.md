### B-518 — Statistical Queries per-person data: a recalculation that produces the same data still saves the person, so every device re-downloads that person — READY · CONFIRMED (code read) · LOW · tier 4 · side finding of B-509 (PR #2438, review round 1, finding 6)
- **Found 2026-10-29** in the review of #2438, which makes the completion scripts skip unchanged saves.
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_reports_calculate_aggregated_data_for_person()` (`hedley_reports.module:510-`) ends with an unconditional write, with no comparison against the stored value:
  ```php
  -- server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_reports/hedley_reports.module:1346-1355 (origin/develop)
    $person = node_load($person->nid, NULL, TRUE);
    ...
    $person->field_reports_data[LANGUAGE_NONE][0]['value'] = $encoded_data;
    node_save($person);
  ```
  Each save gives the person a new revision (`hedley_device_node_presave()`), and devices download every person with a newer revision.
- **Impact.** Unmeasured: how often a queued recalculation yields identical data. Sync load only; report data is correct.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Skip the save when `$encoded_data` equals the reloaded person's stored value, as `hedley_reports_save_completion_data()` does. The `report_data` node write (`:2049-2053`) has the same shape; check its readers first.
