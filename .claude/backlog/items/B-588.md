### B-588 — Prenatal: chronic hypertension or diabetes reported through Outside Care at an earlier visit is not a high-risk condition for preeclampsia — READY · LOW · tier 4 · ⚠ product question · CONFIRMED by reading on PR #2511's head · side-finding of B-586 (review round 1, 2026-11-11)
- **Mechanism.** The earlier-visit triggers read only diagnoses the app made:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm (highRiskPreeclampsiaDiagnosedBySignsAndPreviousDiagnoses, PR #2511)
                  byPreviousChronicHypertension =
                      diagnosedPreviouslyAnyOf chronicHypertensionDiagnoses assembled

                  byPreviousDiabetesDiagnoses =
                      diagnosedDiabetesPrevoiusly assembled
  ```
  `diagnosedPreviouslyAnyOf` reads `data.diagnoses`; an Outside Care diagnosis lands in `pastDiagnoses`, which `diagnosisKnownAnyOf` reads.
- **Effect.** A woman whose chronic hypertension was diagnosed at another facility, recorded in Outside Care, gets no high risk and no Aspirin from it. Before #2511 a later gestational hypertension masked this for hypertension.
- **Spec.** #1529 names Medical History and "a diagnosis from Vitals" / "from lab results" only, so Outside Care not counting is consistent with the text. Ask the program before building.
- **Fix shape (first guess).** Use `diagnosisKnownAnyOf` in both earlier-visit triggers.
