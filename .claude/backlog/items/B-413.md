### B-413 — Every admin content list and CSV export shows soft-deleted content as live, because the views filter `node.status` and nothing ever unpublishes — 159 deleted people still listed on ihangane — READY · CONFIRMED (two scouts converged independently; orchestrator counted live rows) · MED · tier 3 · R34 units 6 + 5 · ⭐ DEPLOYED, all sites
- **Mechanism.** `field_deleted` appears **zero times** in either views export (grep-proved: `hedley_admin.views_default.inc`, 2,794 lines, and `admin_views_default/node.admin-content.inc`, 372 lines). The only liveness filter is publication status, repeated on all 14 displays:
  ```php
  -- server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_admin/hedley_admin.views_default.inc:144-148
    $handler->display->display_options['filters']['status']['id'] = 'status';
    $handler->display->display_options['filters']['status']['table'] = 'node';
    $handler->display->display_options['filters']['status']['field'] = 'status';
    $handler->display->display_options['filters']['status']['value'] = 1;
  ```
  All three soft-delete writers leave `status` alone:
  ```php
  -- server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_admin/hedley_admin.module:45-48
      $wrapper = entity_metadata_wrapper('node', $node);
      $wrapper->field_deleted->set(TRUE);
      $wrapper->save();
  ```
  plus `hedley_patient.drush.inc:261` (patient consolidation) and `hedley_admin/scripts/delete-duplicate-measurements.php:106` (the **daily** Jenkins sweep). No `hook_views_query_alter` exists anywhere in `hedley/modules/custom`. ⛔ This is the standing rule in action: `node.status` is not the liveness test, `field_deleted` is ([[live-queries-must-filter-field-deleted]]).
- **Evidence (orchestrator live query, ihangane.live, 2026-09-21).** Nodes with `field_deleted = 1` **and** `status = 1`: `person` **159**, `relationship` 156, `pmtct_participant` 118, `nutrition` 45, `nutrition_muac` 24, `height` 23, `nutrition_nutrition` 22, `weight` 19, `danger_signs` 16, `attendance` 12, `nutrition_weight` 10, `muac` 9, and more. Every one of these is rendered as live.
- **Reachability.** The site's own admin menu (`hedley_admin.features.menu_links.inc`): `admin/content/children`, `/mothers`, `/measurements`, `/sessions`, `admin/measurement-historical-{child,mother}`, the four `/csv` exports, `admin/ledger/N` and `admin/content`. `admin/content/clinics` and `/nurses` are **not** affected — `clinic` and `nurse` carry no `field_deleted` instance.
- **Impact.** Staff-facing, all sites. A Data Manager who has just run `consolidate-patients` still sees the duplicate child or mother in the list and in the CSV, with nothing marking it as gone; the daily measurement sweep means the Measurements list and its CSV over-report every day. Distinct from [[B-333]], which is about *who* may open these views, not what they show.
- ⭐ **Found twice, independently** — R34 unit 6 (from the views exports) and R34 unit 5 (from `hedley_view_export`, whose AQ CSV inherits the same Master filters). Unit 5 adds that the export half is near-dead ([[B-005]]: 3 exported files on Rwanda ever, newest 2025-06-23, none on Burundi); the **page** half is not.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Add a `field_deleted` filter (`is empty OR = 0`, grouped with the existing `status` filter) to each display that lists a `field_deleted`-bearing bundle. Whether the ledger and the historical views should hide such rows or mark them is a product question.
