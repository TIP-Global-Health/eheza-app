### B-498 — Service worker: a save on the device is reported to every open tab, so each tab runs the assessments and may write its own follow-up or diagnosis — READY · PLAUSIBLE (mechanism read, not reproduced) · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-496 (PR #2377 round 2, finding 2)
- **Mechanism.** `sendRevisions` (`client/src/js/sw.js`) posts `NewRevisions` to `self.clients.matchAll()`, every open client. Each tab's `HandleRevisions RevisionsFromLocalSave` runs the assessments, so two tabs on one encounter can each send SaveFollowUp / a diagnosis PATCH / SaveNutrition for one save.
- **Not introduced by #2377:** on develop every tab already assessed every save outside a download.
- **Impact.** Only with two E-Heza tabs open on one device; tablets rarely do. Duplicate follow-up nodes are the visible form.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Post the echo of a local save only to the client that sent the request (`event.clientId` → `self.clients.get`), and keep a different message for the other tabs that only stores the revision.
