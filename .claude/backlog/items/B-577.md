  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:2712-2749
          DiagnosisHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaInitialPhase ->
              (highRiskPreeclampsiaDiagnosedBySignsAndPreviousDiagnoses

                      (hypertensionDiagnoses ++ diabetesDiagnoses)
              )
                  && (not <| diagnosedHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaPrevoiusly assembled)
                  && ...
          DiagnosisHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaRecurrentPhase ->
              (not <| diagnosedAtInitalPhase DiagnosisHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaInitialPhase)
                  && (highRiskPreeclampsiaDiagnosedBySignsAndPreviousDiagnoses
                          || List.any (...) (hypertensionDiagnoses ++ diabetesDiagnoses)
                     )
                  && (not <| diagnosedHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaPrevoiusly assembled)
                  && ...
  ```
  `hypertensionDiagnoses ++ diabetesDiagnoses` includes the recurrent-only variants (hypertension after recheck, diabetes recurrent), so the initial variant absorbs every case and the recurrent arm is always `False`. The recurrent medication set keys on the variant that cannot exist:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:1990-1995 (resolveRequiredMedicationsSet)
          PrenatalEncounterPhaseRecurrent ->
              Maybe.Extra.values
                  [ resolveHIVPositiveSet DiagnosisHIVRecurrentPhase
                  , resolveDiscordantPartnershipSet DiagnosisDiscordantPartnershipRecurrentPhase
                  , resolvePreeclampsiaRiskSet DiagnosisHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaRecurrentPhase
                  ]
  ```
  `resolvePreeclampsiaRiskSet` (`:1879-1891`) is the only Aspirin path in Prenatal.
- **Reachability.** Nurse encounter at EGA ≥ 12 weeks: marginal BP at the initial phase, then the recheck in the recurrent phase gives hypertension-after-recheck; or a blood sugar / urine glucose result from the lab gives recurrent-phase diabetes.
- 🧪 **Evidence — run.** Repeated BP 145/80 at 28 weeks → `[GestationalHypertensionAfterRecheck, HighRiskOfPreeclampsiaInitialPhase]`; non-immediate blood sugar 200 at 16 weeks → `[DiabetesRecurrentPhase, HighRiskOfPreeclampsiaInitialPhase]` (design in both: `…HighRiskOfPreeclampsiaRecurrentPhase`).
- **Impact.** The recurrent Next Steps offers hypertension treatment only (or nothing, in the diabetes case) — no Aspirin. At every later visit `diagnosedHighRiskOfPreeclampsiaPrevoiusly` is `True`, so the risk is never diagnosed again and Aspirin is never offered for the rest of the pregnancy unless the nurse returns to the finished initial phase's Next Steps the same day.
