### B-648 — Person photo: an older visit photo synced late still replaces a newer photo taken on the person form — READY · LOW · tier 4 · CONFIRMED by reading (review rounds 1 and 2 of PR #2598) · DEPLOYED · side-finding of B-620 · not sized
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_restful_copy_photo_to_person` compares a visit photo only with the person's other photo measurements. A photo set on the person form (registration or edit) is never compared, so a visit photo measured before it but uploaded after it replaces it.
- **Why not fixed in #2598.** The person-form photo has no taken date; its file date is the upload date, which for an offline device is days late, so comparing would block newer visit photos.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Store when the person's photo was taken, or compare against the person node's changed time when the photo field changed.
