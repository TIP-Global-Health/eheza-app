### B-233 — Case Management TB/HIV entries: starting an encounter from an AI-derived TB entry or a positive-test HIV entry creates a SECOND participant for a patient who already has one — ⬆ **widened R31: the AI-derived TB entry survives its own tap, so every tap mints another participant + encounter until the server closure downloads (one ihangane person got 19 in a day)** — READY · CONFIRMED (anchors verified; scout probe for HIV: entry carries `participantId = Nothing` with an HIV participant present) · MED · tier 2 (⬆ from 3, 2026-09-24) · R24 unit 3
- ⬆ **Re-tiered 3 → 2 by the user, 2026-09-24** ("Move all t2 candidates to t2"): the AI-derived TB entry loops, minting a participant + encounter per tap (13 people hold 138 TB participants on ihangane; bursts of 52 in 2025-09 and 19 in 2026-01, none since).
- **Mechanism.** Both entry kinds are built with `Nothing` as participant — TB: `View.elm:837-851` (`TuberculosisFollowUpEntry Nothing personId item True`, from the AI TB-suspect dict, with no check for an existing TB participant); HIV: `View.elm:1023-1049` resolves `resolveIndividualParticipantsForPerson personId HIVEncounter db` to date-check the last encounter, then builds `HIVFollowUpEntry Nothing personId item True` anyway. `Update.elm:157-194` (`startFollowUpEncounterTuberculosis` / `…HIV`) treats `Nothing` as "no participant" and posts `emptyIndividualEncounterParticipant`, which `Backend/Update.elm:~4739-4794` follows with a fresh encounter. No dedupe. The sibling `startFollowUpEncounterHomeVisit` (`Update.elm:114-130`) calls `resolveIndividualParticipantForPerson` first; the program entry points (`Pages/HIV/Participant/View.elm:95-150`) reuse the open participant and navigate to an active encounter.
- **Reachability.** TB (wider): the AI-derived entry exists whenever the TB dict has no live item for the person — all TB follow-ups resolved / `FollowUpNotNeeded`, or B-231's out-of-order case — so a TB patient with a new AI TB-suspect follow-up gets a second TB participant on tap. HIV (narrower): the SW emits the dummy only for persons with NO `hiv_follow_up` node at all (`nodes.js:~645-707`); since the HIV encounter always expects the follow-up task (`Pages/HIV/Activity/Utils.elm:172`), this needs an HIV participant whose encounter(s) never reached next-steps. CHW only; `tuberculosis_management` / `hiv_management`.
- **Impact.** Duplicate `individual_participant` per programme; `resolveIndividualParticipantForPerson` and the Participant pages take `List.head`, so the patient's history splits and which one is opened becomes arbitrary (the B-195 consequence class; the TB comment at `Utils.elm:254-267` assumes one TB participant per patient).
- **Fix shape (guess).** Pass the already-resolved OPEN participant in the HIV branch; in both `startFollowUpEncounter*`, fall back to `resolveIndividualParticipantForPerson` (open only) before creating. Whether to adopt the Participant pages' "navigate to active / block a second today" rules is a product call.
