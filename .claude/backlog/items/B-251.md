### B-251 — Progress-report lab pane: urine-dipstick rows are built from an unfiltered list, so a long-only test also renders a phantom "Short" row and the Long row's Normal/Abnormal verdict can come from a different test — READY · CONFIRMED (anchors verified) · MED · tier 3 · R25 unit 9
- **Mechanism.** In `viewLabResultsPane` (`Pages/Report/View.elm`): `urineDipstickTestResults` (:632-643) takes **every** dipstick value with no `testVariant` filter, while `longUrineDipstickTestResults` (:645-656) filters `value.testVariant == Just VariantLongTest` — there is no short-test counterpart. Then `proteinResults` / `phResults` / `glucoseResults` (:658-665) derive from the **unfiltered** list, and `leukocytes / nitrite / urobilinogen / haemoglobin / ketone / bilirubin` (:667-683) from the **long-only** one.
- **Consequence 1 — a phantom row, on every long-dipstick patient.** `dipstickShortEntry` (:723-770) is driven by `List.head proteinResults`, so a patient who has only ever had a long dipstick still gets a "Urine Dipstick Short" row, dated and graded from that long test. The pane shows one test twice under two names (`Translate.elm:27519-27533` renders them distinctly).
- **Consequence 2 — a wrong verdict.** `dipstickLongEntry` (:772-844) takes its date from the latest **long** test but computes `firstGroupResultsNormal` from the latest dipstick of **either** variant (:797-813). Worked example: long dipstick 10 Jan with `Protein3` (abnormal), short dipstick 10 Feb with `Protein0` → the Long row renders "10/01 — Normal", masking January's abnormal protein. The in-code comment there asserts the assumption that fails: *"We should never get here since leukocytes result existed, and all the results are entered together."* The drill-down panes (:1026-1042) inherit the same mixing.
- **Reachability.** The nurse picks the variant per test (`SetUrineDipstickTestVariant`, `Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Update.elm:2699-2706`, `Pages/NCD/Activity/Update.elm:1105-1111`) and `generateLabsResultsPaneData` aggregates across all prior encounters. Prenatal and NCD progress reports and their WhatsApp exports. Confirmation mode is **not** affected — `dipstickEntries` (:846-857) picks either/or there on current-encounter data only. All sites.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Add a `shortUrineDipstickTestResults` filter symmetric with the long one and derive per-variant protein/pH/glucose lists, so each consolidated entry and each drill-down reads only its own variant. ⚠ Decide what happens to legacy rows whose `testVariant` is `Nothing` (`Backend/Measurement/Decoder.elm:826` defaults it) — today they land in the "short" bucket by accident.
