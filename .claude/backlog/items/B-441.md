### B-441 — Release #2128 post-deploy steps: the head-circumference "not taken" script has no "run again once devices have updated" line, so 0 cm readings old clients keep saving stay flagged as microcephaly — READY · VERIFIED (orchestrator read the PR body, the script, the presave guard and origin/main's warning writer) · LOW · tier 4
- **Found R38 unit 3** (release #2128 contract re-diffed e871d98b6..HEAD). Same class as [[B-341]] and [[B-437]]: a post-deploy step that does not cover everything the field will produce.
- **Mechanism.** `origin/main` (v1.18.2) accepts 0 cm (no minimum: `getInputConstraintsHeadCircumference` is new in #2298), computes the z-score on the device and writes `warning-head-circumference-microcephaly` onto the encounter itself:
```elm
-- origin/main:client/src/elm/Pages/WellChild/Activity/View.elm:816-831
        headCircumferenceZScore =
            if headCircumferenceForm.measurementNotTaken == Just True then
                Nothing
            else
                Maybe.andThen
                    (\headCircumference -> ... zScoreHeadCircumferenceForAge zscores ageInDays assembled.person.gender (Centimetres headCircumference))
                    headCircumferenceForm.headCircumference
```
  The new `hedley_activity` presave guard (#2294) NULLs the server-side z-score for a 0 cm reading but cannot touch the client-written encounter warning. Only `mark-zero-head-circumferences-not-taken.php` corrects both (`field_measurement_notes` → `not-taken`, warning → `no-head-circumference-warning`, `scripts/mark-zero-head-circumferences-not-taken.php:59-67`), and it is idempotent (`isNull('mn.entity_id')` excludes marked rows, `:25-33`). The #2128 body lists it once, with expected counts "5 on ihangane, 15 on vhw", while the #2174 step for the same old-client-tail situation says "Run both again a few weeks later, once devices on older builds have updated".
- **Reachability.** Any device still on v1.18.2 after the deploy, nurse enters 0 for head circumference at a Well Child visit. Real: the 20 targeted records prove nurses do it.
- **Impact.** The child's Well Child progress report and scorecard show a microcephaly warning that was a non-measurement; Statistical Queries count it wherever encounter warnings are read (`hedley_stats.module` reads warnings at ~1635-1675 — B-437 already covers the stats refresh). Magnitude: 20 records over the feature's whole life on two sites, so well under one per site-month during the update window.
