### B-211 — Antenatal progress report: an outside-care HIV diagnosis with "None of these" medication prints as "treated with None of these" — CONFIRMED (orchestrator-read at anchor) · LOW-MED · tier 4 · both sites · R23 unit 1
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Prenatal/ProgressReport/View.elm:3311`: `DiagnosisHIVInitialPhase -> treatedWithPhrase outsideCareMedicationOptionsHIV NoOutsideCareMedicationForMalaria`. The "none" sentinel for HIV is `NoOutsideCareMedicationForHIV` (member of `outsideCareMedicationOptionsHIV`, `Measurement/Utils.elm:~2338`; stored by `SetOutsideCareHIVMedication`). `treatedWithPhrase` (`View.elm:3283-3300`) filters `value.medications` to the option list and emits the "no treatment administered" phrase only when the filtered list is empty or contains the passed sentinel; `[NoOutsideCareMedicationForHIV]` is non-empty and does not match the Malaria sentinel, so it renders as `Translate.OutsideCareMedicationLabel NoOutsideCareMedicationForHIV` = "None of these". Every sibling arm (Syphilis/Malaria/Anemia/Hypertension, `:3327` etc.) passes its own sentinel — copy-adapted arm not updated.
- **Reachability.** Same path as B-210 with HIV + "given medicine" + "None of these".
- **Impact.** Cosmetic-but-misleading line on the progress report and WhatsApp report. No site split.
- **Fix shape (first guess):** pass `NoOutsideCareMedicationForHIV`. Natural to fold into B-210's PR.
