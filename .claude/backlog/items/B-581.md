- **Mechanism.** Hypertension is diagnosed once per pregnancy (`not <| diagnosedHypertensionPrevoiusly assembled`, `Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:2751-2762`); afterwards everything depends on a previous treatment record existing:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:1261-1264, 1362-1363 (resolveHypertensionTreatementUpdateRecommendation)
      Maybe.map2
          (\currentTreatment treatementUpdateByBP -> ...)

  ```
  With no earlier `medicationDistribution` holding a hypertension sign the first argument is `Nothing`, so `updateHypertensionTreatmentWithMedication` is `False` → `continuousHypertensionTreatmentRequired` is `False` unless the earlier diagnosis was moderate preeclampsia (`Activity/Utils.elm:763-791`), and `updateHypertensionTreatmentWithHospitalization` is `False` → `diagnosesCausingHospitalReferralByOtherReasons` adds nothing (`Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:211-233`). The ladder has a `NoTreatmentForHypertension` row (start methyldopa, or hospitalize), reached only when the nurse RECORDED "no treatment"; an absent record falls through. The comment at `:1148-1150` assumes the no-record case exists only for moderate preeclampsia.
- **How a diagnosis ends up without a record.** Hypertension diagnosed together with an emergency-referral diagnosis (Medication Distribution suppressed, `Activity/Utils.elm:502`); hypertension found at the recurrent recheck with the recurrent Next Steps never completed; an initial phase left with Next Steps unfinished (B-205's population).
