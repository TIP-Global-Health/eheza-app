### B-346 — Prenatal HIV / partner HIV form: B-320's "re-tapping the answer already shown changes nothing" guard only works before a reload — after the page is reopened, tapping the "No" already shown under "known as positive" wipes the saved answers from the form and disables Save — READY · CONFIRMED (guard and the update handler read by the orchestrator; scout scratch elm-test) · LOW · tier 4 · R30 unit 2 · ⚠ incomplete guard from #2212 (B-320), merged, NOT deployed; the stored record survives unless the nurse re-enters and saves
- **Mechanism.** The guard compares the tap against the page's RAW form:
  ```elm
  -- client/src/elm/Measurement/Utils.elm:6496-6498
  knownAsPositiveUnchanged knownAsPositive form =
      form.knownAsPositive == Just knownAsPositive
  -- client/src/elm/Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Update.elm:2278-2284
          SetHIVTestFormBoolInput formUpdateFunc value ->
              let
                  form =
                      model.laboratoryData.hivTestForm
                  updatedForm =
                      formUpdateFunc value form
  ```
  The view renders `hivTestFormWithDefault` (raw form + saved value); after a reload the raw form's `knownAsPositive` is `Nothing`, so the guard never fires and the update clears the dependent fields with their dirty flags set.
- **Evidence.** Scout scratch test: displayed before the tap `[Just False, Just True, Just TestNegative, Just True]`, after `[Just False, Nothing, Nothing, Nothing]`. The partner test (`knownAsPositiveUpdatePartnerHIVTest`, `:6557`/`:6585` siblings) behaves the same. B-320's tests use only pre-filled raw forms.
- **Impact.** The nurse loses what the form showed and must re-enter it to save; nothing is written unless she does. Form-refill family (B-274/B-281 class) — not tier 1.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Pass the hydrated form (or the saved value) to the guard / update, so it compares against what the nurse sees.
