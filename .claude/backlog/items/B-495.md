### B-495 — Child Scoreboard NCDA form: a "treated for acute malnutrition" answer survives a MUAC corrected to green or marked not taken, so the hidden question's Yes is still stored — READY · PLAUSIBLE (review round 1 of PR #2369 raised it; orchestrator read the update functions and the value encoder; not reproduced in the app) · LOW · tier 4 · DEPLOYED (Rwanda, `ncda` flag)
- **Found 2026-10-18** in review round 1 of #2369 (B-468), finding 4. Predates that PR.
- **Mechanism.** The question is shown only while `muacMeasurementIsOff form.muac` (`Measurement/View.elm:3448`), and its answer is kept on the form (`:3010`, `treatedForAcuteMalnutrition = Just value`). Correcting the MUAC to a green value, or ticking "not taken" (`:3357`, clears `muac` only), hides the question but leaves the answer, and `toNCDAValueWithDefault` encodes it unconditionally (`Measurement/Utils.elm:9126`, `ifNullableTrue TreatedForAcuteMalnutrition form.treatedForAcuteMalnutrition`). The stored questionnaire then carries the treated sign next to a green or missing MUAC.
- **Impact.** Stored shape only. Since #2369 every reader treats a green or missing MUAC as "not asked" and ignores the sign, so the scorecard is unaffected; the server's aggregated scorecard (`hedley_ncda.module:798`) counts the sign and would count such a child as treated. Live ihangane: the entry for B-468 counted 224 questionnaires with the sign; how many of them sit on a green MUAC is not measured.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Clear `treatedForAcuteMalnutrition` in the MUAC update functions when the new value is green or not taken, the way `ChildWithDisability`'s update clears `receivingSupport`; the Nutrition Assessment step comes before Targeted Interventions, so the normal flow is unchanged.
