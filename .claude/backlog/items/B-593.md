- **Reachability.** Nurse, visit A at 12 weeks or later: random blood sugar sent to the lab. At visit C she enters the result from lab history (`Pages/Prenatal/RecurrentActivity/Update.elm:1647`, `{ form | originatingEncounter = Just originEncounterId }`). C gets the hospital referral for diabetes; neither C nor any later visit gets the risk diagnosis or Aspirin. Entering the same result on A's own Lab Results page does give `HighRiskOfPreeclampsiaRecurrentPhase`.
- **Spec.** #1529 names diabetes "as a diagnosis from lab results" as a high-risk trigger, so unlike B-588 (Outside Care, tier 4, product question) this route is inside the written rule.
