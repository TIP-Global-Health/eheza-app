### B-218 — Person: un-deleting a person does not un-delete what its deletion cascaded (relationships, group enrolments, pregnancies stay deleted) — CONFIRMED (orchestrator-read hook + helper's exclude-deleted filter) · MED-LOW · tier 4 · both sites · R23 unit 6
- **Mechanism.** `hedley_person.module:1271-1294` `hedley_person_process_deleted_update()` — on a `field_deleted` change of a `person` it loads `hedley_person_content_affected_by_deletion()` and sets `field_deleted = $deleted` on each item, symmetrically. But that helper goes through `hedley_general_get_person_content_associated_by_fields()`, which applies `hedley_general_apply_exclude_deleted()` (`hedley_general.module:~253`), so when `$deleted === FALSE` the query returns only content that is ALREADY live — the relationships / pmtct_participants / individual_participants soft-deleted by the earlier deletion are never restored. The `individual_participant` and encounter branches (`:1295`, `:1308`) are explicitly `&& $deleted` only, so even restored participants would not restore their encounters.
- **Reachability.** `field_deleted` on person is an active `options_onoff` widget (`hedley_person.features.field_instance.inc`) — an admin unticking "Deleted" on the node edit form; also the merge-refusal path in presave re-sets it FALSE (no-op there, harmless).
- **Impact.** An admin reversing a wrong deletion or wrong merge (B-217's failure mode) gets the person back with no family, no group enrolments, no pregnancies — the person appears on devices with no history. Adjacent to B-120 (backfill of merge-destroyed links) but a different mechanism: cascade direction, not the merge.
- **Fix shape (first guess):** when `$deleted` is FALSE query WITHOUT the exclude-deleted filter (helper parameter), and decide whether to cascade encounters/measurements symmetrically — or document un-delete as unsupported and hide the widget.
