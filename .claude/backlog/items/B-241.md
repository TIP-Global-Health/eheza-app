### B-241 — Prenatal recurrent Next Steps: choosing a hypertension treatment other than the recommended one raises an unanswerable question and disables Save for good — READY · CONFIRMED (anchors + counting verified) · MED · tier 3 · R25 unit 2
- **Mechanism.** The "why are you not following the recommendation?" input is wired to a dead message at both recurrent call sites — `Pages/Prenatal/RecurrentActivity/View.elm:483` and `Pages/Prenatal/RecurrentActivity/Utils.elm:473`, both passing `(always NoOp)`. The initial phase passes the real `SetAvoidingGuidanceReason` (`Pages/Prenatal/Activity/View.elm:2150`, handler at `Activity/Update.elm:3565`); the recurrent `Msg` type has no such constructor at all. That argument lands in `resolveRecommendedTreatmentForPrevoiuslyDiagnosedHypertensionInputsAndTasks` (`Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:1083-1108`, verified): when the nurse's pick is not the recommended medication it renders six options, reports `taskCompleted form.hypertensionAvoidingGuidanceReason` as completed and **1** as total. Every click dispatches `NoOp`, so completed stays 0 while total is 1.
- **Why it is a hard stop.** View and Utils use the *same* stub, so the counter and the form agree — `saveButton language (tasksCompleted == totalTasks) saveMsg` stays disabled with no way to satisfy it. Reverting to the recommended option is the only escape, and it takes the whole Medication Distribution task with it (the anemia/malaria/syphilis entries live in the same form), so `NextStepsMedicationDistribution` never completes and End Encounter never appears.
- **Reachability.** Recurrent phase of a nurse encounter for a woman with hypertension or moderate preeclampsia diagnosed at a **previous** encounter, where `NextStepsMedicationDistribution` is expected for some other reason — recurrent moderate anemia from the hemoglobin result, recurrent malaria or syphilis, or a non-empty `resolveRequiredMedicationsSet` (`RecurrentActivity/Utils.elm:351-364`). The treatment-update select itself is wired to the live `SetRecommendedTreatmentSign`, so the nurse can always reach the trap.
- **Impact.** Nurses at the recurrent phase are forced to accept the recommended antihypertensive; a clinically justified deviation — stock-out, known allergy, patient declined, cannot afford — can neither be recorded nor worked around, and blocks the encounter. All sites. `ddb2d012a` (2022-05-24) introduced the parameter and stubbed both recurrent callers while wiring the initial phase properly.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Add `SetAvoidingGuidanceReason` to the recurrent `Msg` and `update` mirroring the initial-phase handler (including the `hypertensionAvoidingGuidanceReasonDirty` flag and the clear inside `SetRecommendedTreatmentSign`), and wire both call sites. If the question is genuinely not wanted at the recurrent phase, the alternative is to suppress the derived section there rather than count it.
- 📌 Same-unit divergence, traced and **inert**, recorded so it is not re-chased: `RecurrentActivity/Update.elm`'s `SetPartnerHIVTestResult` sets only `testResult` where the initial-phase twin also clears `partnerTakingARV`/`partnerSurpressedViralLoad`. Every reader of those signs gates on `testResult == TestPositive` first (`Pages/Prenatal/Utils.elm:1829`, `:4523`, `Activity/Utils.elm:2308-2335`, `ProgressReport/View.elm:1004`), so the stale sign is unreachable.
