### B-257 — Duplicate-patient lookup is triggered by any text field over 13 characters and sized to Rwanda's ID, so the duplicate warning is effectively Rwanda-only — READY · CONFIRMED (anchor + per-site validators verified) · LOW · tier 4 · R25 unit 5
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Person/Update.elm:142-148` matches `Form.Input _ _ (Form.Field.String value)` and fires `FetchPeopleByNationalId value` whenever `String.length value > 13` — **the field name is discarded**. So every keystroke past 13 characters in *any* text field (spouse name, next-of-kin name, and the photo URL pushed by `DropZoneComplete`, :171-189) issues a `national_id_contains` IndexedDB prefix scan (`Backend/Update.elm:1620`, `nodes.js:968-988`) and caches the result under that string forever.
- **Two consequences.** (a) Junk keys accumulate in `personSearchesByNationalId`. (b) The threshold assumes an ID of at least 14 characters, but the validator branches per site: `Backend/Person/Form.elm:419-429` requires exactly 16 digits for Rwanda and accepts a bare `nullable string` for `SiteBurundi` / `SiteSomalia` (the Somalia arm still carries `-- @todo - needs validation?`). On any site whose national ID is 13 characters or fewer, no search is ever issued, so the lookup at submit (`Update.elm:71-88`) always misses and the duplicate-suspect dialog never appears.
- **Also in the same lookup:** Rwanda's validator trims the ID while the Burundi/Somalia arm does not, yet the search key *is* trimmed (`Backend/Update.elm:1622`) — so a leading or trailing space makes the lookup miss on those sites.
- ⚠ **Unverified and it is what sizes the item:** the real national-ID lengths used in Burundi and Somalia. If they are 14+ characters the "never fires" half does not apply.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Match on the national-ID field name rather than on any string, and take the trigger length from the site's own ID rule.
