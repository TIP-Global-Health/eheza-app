     - ⭐ **Why the suggested fix does not work, so nobody rebuilds it:** the reviewer proposed checking the verdict's milestone against the milestone due at the selected month (`resolvePediatricCareMilestoneOnDate`). That test **cannot separate the two causes of a missing verdict** — (A) the child completed every sign expected at their age, so the activity was not offered and the old verdict still holds, from (B) signs were outstanding and the nurse skipped the activity. Both look identical to a milestone comparison, and (A) is precisely the case this PR exists to stop painting red. Separating them needs the child's completed ECD signs, and **the dashboard payload does not carry them** — `SPVEncounterDataItem` (`Backend/Dashboard/Model.elm:341`, decoder `:382`) has warnings, z-scores, MUAC, nutrition signs and 9 immunisation date sets, and nothing server-side supplies ECD signs. I recommended this fix before checking the payload; that recommendation was wrong.
