### B-438 — Patient merge: TB and HIV episodes of the duplicate are folded into its OLDEST participant, and the open episode is deleted — READY · VERIFIED (orchestrator read handler, validator, loader order, TB/HIV close paths) · MED-LOW · tier 3 (latent — an admin merge of a person with several TB/HIV episodes)
- **Found R37 unit 4** (person / case-management cluster as merged, @e747d3eaf). Sibling of [[B-375]] / [[B-376]].
- **Mechanism.** TB and HIV are merged by the handler for programs that have only one participant per person (`_consolidate_tuberculosis_encounter_content_execute` / `_consolidate_hiv_encounter_content_execute` → `_consolidate_single_participant_content_execute`, `hedley_patient.drush.inc:1104`, `:1146`). But both programs close a participant (`CloseTuberculosisSession` / `CloseHIVSession` in `Backend/IndividualEncounterParticipant/Update.elm:49-75`, e.g. TB "not diagnosed"), and a later suspicion opens a new one.
```php
// server/hedley/modules/custom/hedley_patient/hedley_patient.drush.inc:1235-1270
```

  - The list is ordered `n.nid DESC` (`hedley_person_individual_participants_for_person`, `hedley_person.module:1359-1371`), so `array_pop` takes the **oldest** participant, not the "last" one the comment names.
  - Duplicate with an ended TB-1 and an open TB-2, original with none: TB-1 moves to the original, TB-2's encounters are moved under TB-1, and TB-2 is soft-deleted. The original has no open TB case (`resolveOpenParticipant` filters on `endDate`, so the app offers to start a new one), and the current episode sits under a closed participant with the old outcome.
  - When the original has one participant, every duplicate episode, ended or open, is folded into it.
  - Validation (`:1191-1196`) refuses only when the ORIGINAL has more than one participant; the duplicate side is unchecked.
- **Reachability.** `admin/content/consolidate-patients` or `drush consolidate-patients`; the TB and HIV steps run on every merge.
- **Live (scout, ihangane.live, read-only).** Persons with 2 or more non-deleted participants of one type: TB 13 (9 with both an ended and an open one), HIV 1. The defect fires only when such a person is merged as the duplicate.
- **Prior coverage.** The R32 row calls the single-participant handlers clean; it did not consider that TB and HIV have several episodes.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Handle TB and HIV like acute illness, by re-pointing every duplicate participant to the original. At minimum, refuse the merge when the duplicate has more than one participant of the type, or pop the newest.
