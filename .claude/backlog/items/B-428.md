### B-428 — Aggregated NCDA scoreboard (percentage mode): the Breastfed, complementary-feeding, diverse-diet, meals-a-day and both ANC rows divide an age-restricted or pregnancy-month numerator by every child under two, so they cannot reach 100 % — READY · CONFIRMED (orchestrator read the shared denominator, each pane's numerator and the one pane that already uses its own denominator) · MED-LOW · tier 3 · R36 unit 2 · DEPLOYED (Rwanda admin report)

