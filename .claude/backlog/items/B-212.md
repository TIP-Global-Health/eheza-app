### B-212 — Antenatal progress report: NCD-program referral lines are shown/hidden by ARV-program enrolment, not NCD enrolment — ✅ IMPLEMENTED · CONFIRMED (orchestrator-read at anchor + sibling) · MED · tier 3 · both sites · R23 unit 1
- **Mechanism.** `Pages/Prenatal/ProgressReport/View.elm:918-948` (`programReferralEntries`): the ARV block tests `EverySet.member EnrolledToARVProgram value`; the NCD block directly below (`ncdEntries`, `:935-944`) tests **the same** `EnrolledToARVProgram` instead of `EnrolledToNCDProgram`. The live sibling in the activity keys NCD on `EnrolledToNCDProgram` (`Pages/Prenatal/Activity/Utils.elm:6893`). Result: a patient with a prior hypertension/diabetes diagnosis who answered "enrolled in NCD program" (so no NCD referral was made) but is not in an ARV program gets a "Hypertension – referred to NCD program on DD/MM/YYYY" line; a patient in ARV but not in NCD has the NCD referral line suppressed.
- **Reachability.** Nurse → subsequent prenatal encounter where Speciality Care is expected (previous hypertension-like or diabetes diagnosis) → save `specialityCare` → Progress Report → Medical Diagnosis pane.
- **Impact.** Wrong referral history on the clinical document; both sites.
- **Secondary (same block, note only):** `resolveARVReferralDiagnosis`/`resolveNCDReferralDiagnoses` are fed the full `assembled.nursePreviousEncountersData` for every past encounter, not the encounters before `data.startDate` (compare `updateChronicHypertensionDiagnoses`, which filters with `filterPreviousEncountersDataToDate`) — the diagnosis label on an old referral line can come from a later encounter. Low impact.
- **Fix shape (first guess):** test `EnrolledToNCDProgram` in the NCD branch.
