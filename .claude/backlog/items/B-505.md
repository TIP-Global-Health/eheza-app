### B-505 — Sync download (general): two copies of one general request that both reply before the first batch's save is acknowledged are both accepted, so the batch is saved and processed twice — READY · CONFIRMED (code read on B-436's branch `c215085fe`) · LOW · tier 4 · side-finding of B-436 review round 2 ([comment on #2407](https://github.com/TIP-Global-Health/eheza-app/pull/2407#discussion_r4336453007)) · pre-existing on develop

  Unlike the authority save handler, it has no `downloadAuthorityAtRequest`-style check of which request the saved batch answered.
- **Impact.** When the first acknowledgement leaves the cycle in the general lane (a full batch), the second one runs `ResetFailedToFetchAuthorities`, `handleNewRevisionsMsg` and `sendLocalIdsForDelete` again for the same entities, and sets the cursor to the same value. All of it repeats work on the same data; the worst case is one extra fetch. Needs a reply slower than 30 seconds, so slow links only.
- **Fix shape (guess).** Accept a general reply only while no batch from the same base revision is waiting for its save (or record the base revision of the saved batch and check it on acknowledgement, as the authority lane does with `downloadAuthorityAtRequest`).
