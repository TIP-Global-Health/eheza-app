- **Why not in B-537.** B-537's `drush merge-duplicate-participants` covers only the one-per-person programmes. HIV/TB participants are episodes, and three things differ (B-537 review round 2, findings 3–5):
  1. Devices upload participant edits for these types (closing an episode via `CloseTuberculosisSession` / `CloseHIVSession`), and the participant encoder sends `deleted: false`, so an offline device can bring a merged participant back to life.
  2. Lowest nid follows upload order, not episode start: merging can attach encounters to an episode that starts after them (treatment-week and date maths).
  3. A late encounter on a merged open participant has no single right target once the survivor episode has been closed and a new one opened.
